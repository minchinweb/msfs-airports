# H28 reference images

- `wikimedia-sign-and-runway.jpg`: "Frank Whetstone International Airport.jpg" by FiveXdollars, CC0, 2026-05-09 (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Frank_Whetstone_International_Airport.jpg), downscaled to 1200x1600. At the west end by the road: wooden sign "Frank Whetstone International Airport" on white posts; barbed-wire fence along the road; white cones with black tops (a large one by the fence, a row of smaller ones down the runway edges); orange ball markers on the power line; a wooden pole with a transformer and a line running across the strip; the windsock (red/orange) stands well down the strip, left of the pole; flat green grass. Windsock position still to be matched.
- `sim-aerial-west-end-184446.png`: sim aerial screenshot (2026-10-27) of the west end, showing the striped runway ending at the property edge. This is the file attached as the "west end photo"; it is the same image as the aerial screenshot, not a ground photo.
- `streetview-west-end-2025-09.png`: Google Street View, Del Bonita AB-62, Sep 2025, looking east from the road onto the strip (the photo meant by "west end"). Shows the road-edge fence and sign (left), a white cone near the fence, a gate/post and a power pole with a transformer on the strip, a tall streetlight pole (right), the windsock far down the strip (left of centre), white cones along the strip and the port-of-entry signs at right.
- `earth-2012-west-parking.jpg`: Google Earth 2012, 30 m scale bar (about 8.9 px/m), north up, yellow line = border. West fence about 13 m east of the road. Mowed strip north of the line, about 37 m wide, with its south edge on the line. Parking is a fenced rectangle south of the line (about 140 m x 78 m). Power-pole shadow on the line about 33 m east of the west fence; windsock shadow on the line at the parking's east edge, about 138 m east of the fence. White cones along the line about every 30 m.
- `earth-2012-west-end-wide.jpg`: Google Earth 2012, 80 m scale bar (about 4.1 px/m). Port-of-entry buildings north-west across the road. First white cones about 75-130 m east of the fence; red-white cones (pairs north and south of the strip) about 200 m and 300 m east of the fence. The red-white cones mark the effective runway end (displaced threshold?), with white cones marking the rest.

- `afd-whetstone-h28-page-136.md`: transcription of the Northwest U.S. Airport/Facility Directory page 136 entry for Whetstone Intl (H28), effective 29 Oct 2026 (https://xcfind.com/static/afd/NW_136_29OCT2026.pdf). Gives RWY 07-25 as 4440 x 65 ft turf, CTAF 122.8, thresholds double cones, edge white cones every 200 ft, red balls on the P-line.
