# R6 Quesnell Bridge — sources

Photos in `photos\` are openly licensed (CC BY-SA). A file that was useful but not openly licensed, or that is CC BY-NC, is listed under "Not downloaded" with the URL only. The 2008 elevation is the pre-widening deck. The 2015 river frames are after the 2011 opening.

| [City of Edmonton / CH2M HILL, PIEVC climate-change vulnerability assessment (PDF)](https://pievc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/appendix_b.5_city_of_edmonton_quesnell_bridge_refurbishment.pdf) | 315 m. Six spans: 50 m simple, three-span continuous 164.7 m, two-span continuous 53.3 m and 46.6 m. Ten lines of parabolic precast prestressed post-tensioned girders, cast-in-place deck. Original 1968: five lanes, two southbound, three northbound, 3.0 m outside shoulders, 0.6 m inside shoulders, 4.5 m east sidewalk. Then-current 2008: six lanes, 4.5 m sidewalk. Finished design: eight lanes, emergency shoulder each way, 4.2 m walkway. Five piers, two shafts each, post-tensioned caps, spread footings in bedrock. South abutment steel H-piles, north abutment bell piles, 3.2 m approach slabs. Elastomeric bearings. Fox Drive ramp merges on the south of this deck. Widening: replace deck, add and strengthen girders, two extra girders each way at the caps, lengthen pier caps. Transport Canada named for clearance under NWPA; no clearance figure. Edmonton elevation 668 m, the city, not the deck. | Engineers Canada / PIEVC case-study PDF. Text used. Exhibit 2-4, the plan and elevation, was not transcribed.
