# W85 Fox Drive, Campbell Bridge — modeling brief

Edmonton. Road bridge on Fox Drive NW over Whitemud Creek, at the north edge of Whitemud Park. The present structure was built by the City in 1969 (Edmonton Nature Centres Foundation walk sheet; edmonton.name repeats that sentence). Centre used by the project: 53.5032, -113.5610. This crossing was left off the freeway list. It sits beside Quesnell, not on Whitemud Drive.

The south carriageway is eastbound. Way 25475831 runs from 53.503126, -113.561549 to 53.503063, -113.560214, west to east. The north carriageway is westbound. Ways 25475833 and 944470767 run from 53.503148, -113.560201 to 53.503214, -113.561543, east to west. Each road way is tagged three lanes, 70 km/h, `bridge:name=Campbell Bridge`. Wikipedia's Fox Drive article uses that name for this creek crossing.

This is not R6 Quesnell Bridge, centre 53.507, -113.567. Quesnell is the eight-lane Whitemud river span. Do not copy its 315 m, 319.8 m, or 333 m lengths, its span list, or its pier notes here. It is not W87 Rainbow Valley, 53.483, -113.555. It is not R8 Groat. It is not H36 Henday / Whitemud west. It is not the Whitemud-over-Fox-Drive interchange structure named in the 2004 Earth Tech title beside Quesnell. The extract's first way, 25475768, is a footway about 170 m south of this deck. That trail is not this bridge.

Numbers below are cited. Where two sources disagree, both are given. A blank in the "Still missing" list has no published figure in the sources checked. Nothing here was filled in by adding one citation to another. OpenStreetMap `width=11.1` is a mapper tag on the road ways. It is not a published structural width. There is no `maxheight` tag on these bridge ways. A mapper tag is not a deck elevation.

## Bridge

**Type.** Not published beyond a road bridge over Whitemud Creek. The OpenStreetMap road ways have no `bridge:structure` tag. The 2 August 2012 photograph, taken from Fort Edmonton Park Road looking east, shows the deck, a metal railing, lamp posts, and a 70 km/h sign. That frame is not a truss. A girder, beam, or slab name was not found.

**Spans.** Individual span lengths are not published. A span count is not published. They were not made by dividing a mapped length.

**Length.** No published structural length was found. Structurae has no Edmonton Campbell Bridge page in the sources checked. The OpenStreetMap south centreline, way 25475831, is 88.7 m. The north through ways, 25475833 then 944470767, are 67.1 m and 22.1 m. That is 89.2 m if the two are placed end to end. The north path, way 27265227, is 89.5 m. The south sidewalk, way 582712471, is 89.3 m. Use those lines for the plan shape. The mapped length and a structural length between abutments are different extents if a structural length is ever published.

Wikipedia's Fox Drive length is 2.2 km. That is the road from Whitemud Drive to Belgravia Road / 122 Street. It is not this span.

**Girders.** Not published. Girder depth is not published. A line count is not published. Depth was not read from the 2012 photograph. That frame does not show the soffit.

**Piers.** Not published. A pier count is not published. The Edmonton Nature Centres Foundation sheet and edmonton.name say walkers can pass under Campbell Bridge on the park road. They do not describe the supports. A count was not taken from any photograph.

**Clearance above the creek.** Not published. No navigation clearance is published. No elevation above sea level is published for the deck, the soffit, or the water. Environment Canada gauge 05DF001 is at the Low Level Bridge and uses a local assumed datum. No source used here converts a clearance at this creek from that gauge.

**Name and year.** Named for Duncan L. Campbell, who farmed the area and built the first bridge over the creek. The present bridge was built by the City in 1969 (Edmonton Nature Centres Foundation; edmonton.name). Naming Edmonton has a Fox Drive entry, designated 1968, for Thomas Fox. It has no Campbell Bridge entry in the sources checked. The 1968 year is the road name. It is not used here as the span year.

**Rehabilitation that shows.** Canada Gazette Part I, second quarter 2008, indexes a notice: City of Edmonton, rehabilitation of the Campbell Bridge over Whitemud Creek. The index does not describe the work. What that rehabilitation changed on the silhouette is not published in the sources checked. The 2 August 2012 photograph is later than that index line.

## Deck

**Elevation.** 2018 LiDAR, CGVD2013. DSM median along the south carriageway, way 25475831: **628.20 m** (p10 628.02 m, p90 628.39 m, 28 samples). Along the north through ways: 25475833 **628.12 m**, 944470767 **627.88 m**. Along the north path, way 27265227: **628.68 m**. Along the south sidewalk, way 582712471: **628.91 m**. Those medians were not averaged. Water surface in this clip: **618.09 m**, valid. The clip holds a water reach at the Whitemud Creek mouth. South carriageway median 10.11 m above that water. DTM at the project centre (53.5032, -113.5610): **618.37 m**. That centre sits over the creek, so the DTM there is the water, not a bank. DSM is first-return; traffic and railings can add up to 1 m. Rise is DSM minus the water surface, not a published clear height. Subtract deck depth if a clearance is stated. No published geodetic deck elevation was found to set beside these figures. The Quesnell LiDAR medians near 637.8 m are the river span. They are not used here.

**Thickness.** Not published. The depth from the wearing surface to the soffit is not published.

**Width.** OpenStreetMap tags `width=11.1` on both road decks. No published source found states 11.1 m. Lane width is missing. Shoulder width is missing. A published overall width was not found. The two road centrelines are a few metres apart. That offset is not a published width.

**Lanes.** Three each way, asphalt (OpenStreetMap; Wikipedia's 70 km/h Fox Drive). Each road way is tagged 70 km/h and `oneway=yes`. A live read of the same road ways on 14 October 2026 also has `expressway=yes`, `lit=yes`, `hgv=no`, and `hgv:conditional=yes @ (Mo-Sa 07:00-22:00)`. The north ways also have `psv:lanes=||designated` and `cycleway:left=share_busway`. The extract does not store those tags. The tags are not a deck elevation.

**Sidewalks.** One mapped sidewalk on the south, way 582712471, concrete footway, 89.3 m, tagged `footway=sidewalk` on the live read, layer 1. One mapped shared-use path on the north, way 27265227, concrete cycleway, 89.5 m, foot and bicycle designated, layer 2. A live read of that path also has `segregated=no`. The road ways are tagged `sidewalk:right=separate` and `sidewalk:left=no`. The south way runs west to east, so its right side is the south side. The north ways run east to west, so their right side is the north side. A sidewalk width is not published. Width was not measured from the 2012 photograph.

**Crossfall.** Not published. Longitudinal grade on the span is not published.

## Still missing

- Clearance from the roadway or the soffit down to the creek.

- A published structural type beyond a road bridge. Girder depth. Number of girder lines.

- Deck thickness, and the depth from the wearing surface to the soffit.
- Lane width and shoulder width. What the OpenStreetMap `width=11.1` tag measures. A published structural width. Sidewalk width.
- Crossfall as a slope. Longitudinal grade on the spans.
- Where each Fox Drive approach meets natural grade, and the length, gradient, and elevation change of each climb.
- What the 2008 Gazette rehabilitation changed on the silhouette.
- An openly licensed underside or elevation that shows the piers. An openly licensed aerial. City 2019 Pictometry clips in `aerial/` are measuring reference only (licence not confirmed).
