# Y79 Bremner Cloverleaf — modeling brief

Strathcona County, immediately east of Edmonton city limits, at Bremner. Highway 21 over Highway 16 (Yellowhead Highway). Two overpass decks and cloverleaf loops. Centre used by the project: 53.5702, -113.2461. The VFR checkpoint Bremner Cloverleaf is VCBCL, N53° 34.30′ W113° 14.70′. Carries Highway 21, three lanes each way on the overpasses. Highway 16 runs under them, two lanes each way.

OpenStreetMap way 32829979 is the east overpass. Node order is from 53.569807, -113.24613 to 53.570581, -113.246074, south to north, so the one-way is northbound. Way 153737445 is the west overpass. Node order is from 53.570639, -113.246551 to 53.569822, -113.246603, north to south, so the one-way is southbound.

## Bridge

**Type.** Two separate grade-separation overpasses. Alberta Transportation file 75760, Highway 21 Over Highway 16 Interchange, 5 km NE of Sherwood Park.

N-1, on Highway 21 right (northbound), east of the median. Inspection 4 January 2013: FC, three spans, 15 girders, allowable-load line GIRDER, special feature EXT LATER POST TENS. Concrete abutments. Piers typed PIER-COLUMN. Form type PSR. No sidewalk.

S-6, on Highway 21 left (southbound), west of the median. Inspection 4 January 2013: WG, two spans, weathering steel, form type SG. Concrete abutments. Piers typed PIER-COLUMN. ACP wearing surface 90 mm. No sidewalk.

The 2011–13 provincial note names "Highway 21 Bridge over Highway 16 near Sherwood Park" and a $0.9-million girder repair and deck joint replacement. It does not say which of the two decks. The OpenStreetMap overpass ways have no `bridge:structure` tag.

**Spans.** N-1 inspection: lengths 29 m, 16.2 m, and 29 m. The inventory row prints GS 3, 29.0, and 74.2. S-6 inspection: lengths 38.65 m and 38.65 m. The inventory row prints GS 2, 38.7, and 77.3. The 29 m, the 16.2 m, the 38.65 m, the 74.2 m, and the 77.3 m are all published. They were not added here and they were not averaged.

**Length.** No source other than those inspection and inventory figures states a length between abutments. The OpenStreetMap east centreline, way 32829979, is 86.2 m. The west centreline, way 153737445, is 91.0 m. Use those lines for the plan shape. The mapped lengths and the inspection span totals are different extents.

**Girders.** N-1: 15 girders (2013 inspection). Girder depth is not published. S-6: weathering steel, webs, flanges, stiffeners, and cross-frames named on the inspection form. A girder-line count for S-6 is not published. Depth was not read from any photograph. No openly licensed elevation was found.

**Piers.** N-1: two piers for three spans. The inspection names P1 and P2 and the north and south abutments. Bearing seats typed PIER-COLUMN, eight columns, "possibly 2 piles per column." S-6: one pier for two spans, typed PIER-COLUMN, five bearing piles on the pier line. Pier height is not published. A footing plan size is not published.

**Clearance over Highway 16.** Alberta's vertical-clearances table: Hwy. 21 at Bremner, east 5.3 m, west 5.3 m. N-1 inspection required posting under 16 L1 5.3 m and 16 R1 5.3 m, and posted 5.3 m. S-6 inspection required posting under 16 L1 5.5 m and 16 R1 5.5 m, and posted 5.3 m eastbound, with the remark "5.3m Clearance for Overpass to South." The 5.3 m and the 5.5 m are both published. They were not averaged. They are clearances under the overpass. They are not deck elevations.

OpenStreetMap way 61014013 (Yellowhead, dropped from this geometry) tags `maxheight=5.3`. Way 1099451495 tags `maxheight=5.2`. No published source found states 5.2 m for this overpass. The vertical-clearances table's "C.N.R. at Bremner" line is 5.4 m east and 5.2 m west. That is file 73615. It is not this deck.

**Opening and widening.** Highway 21 reached Highway 16 in the 1970s (Wikipedia, citing the 1978/79 official map). N-1 year built 1967 (inventory and inspection). S-6 year built 2008 (inventory and inspection). Sherwood Park News, 8 October 2009: the Highway 16 interchange was being improved with updated access points and a second overpass for southbound traffic; twinning expected open mid-November 2009, weather permitting. Wikipedia, citing that article, says the Sherwood Park section was completed in late 2009. The 2008 year and the 2009 opening are both published. They were not averaged.

The same 2009 article says another update into a modified four-leaf-clover interchange is on the province's long-term project agenda. Present-day package: model the interchange that stands now, including the loops. Do not invent a later rebuild.

Alberta 22 September 2006: twinning from south of Highway 628 to north of Highway 16 includes the interchange upgrade at Highways 16 and 21. Cost of the twinning project $81.3 million (2009 article). About 10,000 vehicles a day on this section of Highway 21 (2006 release). Inspection AADT 10,770 in 2011.

**Rehabilitation that shows.** 2011–13: girder repair and deck joint replacement, $0.9 million (Alberta recent-bridge-projects note; 2012–15 program lists 75760-1, Bridge Rehabilitation). The N-1 inspection of January 2013 already notes joint replacement at an abutment in progress. What that work changed on the silhouette is not described beyond girders and deck joints. The 2018 LiDAR is after that contract window.

## Deck

**Elevation.** 2018 LiDAR, CGVD2013. DSM median along the east overpass, way 32829979: **677.27 m** (p10 677.21 m, p90 677.31 m, 29 samples). Along the west overpass, way 153737445: **677.70 m** (p10 677.62 m, p90 677.72 m, 30 samples). Those medians were not averaged. DTM at the project centre (53.5702, -113.2461): **669.77 m**. That centre sits in the interchange, over Highway 16, so the DTM there is the ground under the overpasses, not a river. DSM is first-return; traffic and railings can add up to 1 m. Rise is DSM minus that DTM, not a published clear height. Subtract deck depth if a clearance is stated. No published geodetic deck elevation was found to set beside these figures. The clip does not hold a river reach. Gauge 05DF001 was not converted.

**Thickness.** N-1 inspection: wearing surface conventional chip seal, thickness 50 mm, with a 2 m concrete-to-grade strip. S-6 inspection: ACP 90 mm. The depth from the wearing surface to the soffit is not published. Girder depth is not published.

**Width.** N-1 inspection: clear roadway 14.2 m, roadway width 14.300 m, skew 2 deg RHF. S-6 inspection: clear roadway 16.6 m, roadway width 17.200 m, skew 2 deg RHF. Lane width is missing. Shoulder width is missing. The two overpass centrelines are 31.3 m apart at the south end and 32.2 m apart at the north end. Those are distances between mapped centrelines. They are not a published width.

**Lanes.** Three on each overpass, one-way, asphalt, 80 km/h (OpenStreetMap). Highway 16 underneath is two lanes each way, 110 km/h. N-1 and S-6 inspections both list sidewalk as none. No path ways are tagged on these overpasses.

**Crossfall.** Not published. Longitudinal grade on the spans is not published. The inspections call the alignments a typical grade separation with merging on and off ramps.

## Added 2026-10-15 (read while modelling)

- The interchange is a partial cloverleaf: two loops, both east of Highway 21 (NE loop 32834983 and SE loop 32835223), with four outer ramps. Every ramp is now in `geometry.json`.
- The ground is low and flat (about 670.5 m); Highway 21 and the ramps are on embankment up to about 7 m (bare earth 677 m at the decks and approaches, 670 to 671 m where the loops reach Highway 16). Loop side slopes read as about 4:1 from the DTM.
- The N-1 "15 girders" is most likely 5 lines in each of 3 spans (5 x 3), which fits a 14.3 m deck. This is a reading, not a published statement.
- The 5.3 m posted clearance over Highway 16 (DTM 670.0 to 670.3 m) puts the soffit near 675.6 m, so deck top (677.2 m) minus soffit is about 1.6 m on N-1 and about 2.1 m on S-6. That is the only constraint on girder depth.
- The LiDAR DTM under the abutments reads as ground below the deck (0 to 5 m) for the last 10 m, so the approach heights near the decks come from the first returns.
- The two Highway 21 carriageways are about 31 to 33 m apart (grass median).
