# G119 107 Avenue — modeling brief

107 Avenue NW over Groat Road. Glenora Overpass. Centre used by the project: 53.5510, -113.5498. Alberta file B097, also printed as file 76536. Year built 1967.

## What it is

Two carriageways of 107 Avenue, one bridge. The south carriageway is eastbound. The north carriageway is westbound. A sidewalk lies outside each carriageway.

This is not the river span. Do not reuse the river span's lengths, the east path width, or the pier notes. The EIA says this crossing is just beyond the north end of the ravine. It gives no length and no height.

## Bridge

**Type.** Grade separation. Alberta's row, GLENORA OVERPASS OVER GROAT ROAD, prints year 1967 and FC MAJOR BRIDGE GS 3. Read the 3 as three spans on the same pattern as the Mayfair row on that report and the Groat river row. The other numbers on the row were not assigned. A published span length was not found.

**Rehabilitation.** The Bridge Renewal Program table in council document 126658 says a full rehabilitation, with planned completion in 2022. Document 119895 says construction was completed in 2021. Both are kept. Update document 126655 says the Glenora rehabilitation, 107 Avenue over Groat Road, was underway ahead of the Stony Plain work that was to start in late 2022. The same update says the 102 Avenue replacement was completed in 2016 and that Wellington was planned after Stony Plain. What the 2021 or 2022 work changed on the girders, the piers, or the railings is not described.

**Spans.** The printed count is 3. Individual span lengths were not found.

**Length.** Not published. Mapped carriageways, horizontal WGS84:

- Way 22928017, south carriageway, two lanes, one-way, asphalt, 75.8 m. Node order west to east, so eastbound.
- Way 158414123, north carriageway, two lanes, one-way, asphalt, 75.3 m. Node order east to west, so westbound.

The two lengths were not averaged. Neither is a structural length.

The outline, way 1510405388, tags bridge:structure=beam. Its perimeter is 224.7 m. That is not a span. The long edges are 73.7 m. The short edges are 38.7 m and 38.6 m. Those are mapped edges. They are not a published width. The end nodes of the two road centrelines are 16.4 m apart at the west end and 16.7 m apart at the east end. That separation is not a published width.

## Approaches

107 Avenue leaves the bridge tag as short two-lane one-way pieces: way 472632850, 22.3 m; way 472632840, 21.4 m; way 472632842, 7.5 m; way 472632845, 5.1 m. The far node of each is not a surveyed grade point. No gradient is published.

Groat Road underneath: way 98093800, 611.2 m, maxheight tag 4.8; way 157319879, 614.5 m, maxheight tag 4.9. No published source found states 4.8 m or 4.9 m. The tags are not a clearance under this deck and not a deck elevation. The ways continue well past the bridge.

## Deck

**Elevation.** Not published. Gauge 05DF001 was not converted.

**Lanes.** Two carriageways, each tagged two lanes and one-way, 50 km/h. Eastbound on the south carriageway. Westbound on the north carriageway. Four lanes if both are open. A published lane count, apart from those tags, was not found.

**Sidewalks.** Way 114768445, concrete, foot yes, 74.1 m, south of the south carriageway. Way 159377842, concrete, foot yes, 75.5 m, north of the north carriageway. A live read of the north carriageway has sidewalk:left=no and sidewalk:right=separate, and hgv=designated on both carriageways. Those tags are not elevations. No published sidewalk width was found.
