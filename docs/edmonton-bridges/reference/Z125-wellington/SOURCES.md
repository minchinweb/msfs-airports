| [City of Edmonton, Wellington Bridge](https://www.edmonton.ca/projects_plans/roads/wellington-bridge) | 102 Avenue between 130 Street and 132 Street. Built 1932. Four lanes, two each way. Sidewalk on each side. Last rehabilitated more than 25 years ago. Shoring since 2014. Crosses Ramsey Ravine. Replacement started the week of 15 September 2025. March 2026 update: completion anticipated at the end of 2026, landscaping in 2027. Finished design on the page: clear-span girder, some historical elements at roadway level, shared pathway on the north, sidewalk on the south, wider lanes, barriers, handrails, decorative streetlights. Girder chosen over the concrete arch because of cost, foundations, and schedule. Closed to all traffic, including the trail underneath. The 2023 update, when the recommendation was still a concrete arch, says a 4.2 m shared-use path on the north and a 2.8 m sidewalk on the south. The March 2026 girder text does not restate those widths. | City of Edmonton. Text used. Page photographs were not downloaded. |
| [EIA amendment, Associated Engineering, 8 August 2024 (PDF)](https://pub-edmonton.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=239816) | Three-span concrete arch, 1932, shoring since 2014, Ramsey Ravine, between Wellington Crescent and Churchill Crescent, City bridge B003. May 2021 options: single-span steel girder, three-span haunched concrete, modern concrete arch. Arch design paused February 2022 and restarted January 2024. August 2024: design being updated to a clear-span girder. Steel and concrete girders both considered. Permanent footprint should be reduced from the April 2024 designs because the piers are eliminated. Construction footprint unchanged.
