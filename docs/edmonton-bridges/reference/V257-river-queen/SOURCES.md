# Edmonton River Queen — sources

Photos in `photos\` are openly licensed (CC BY or CC BY-SA). A file that was useful but not openly licensed is listed under "Not downloaded" with the URL only. `elevation.jpg`, `south-bank.jpg`, and `mooring-2018.jpg` are 1,920 px Wikimedia thumbnails. The full-size originals were not fetched. `stern-wheels.jpg` and `paddlewheel.jpg` are the full files. The originals are 2,048 px and 1,730 px wide.

The photographs show the hull that is still registered as official number 817706. The 2020 puncture and the 2022 repair are later than the 2008, 2010, and 2012 frames. No source describes a new hull outline after that repair. The 18 June 2018 frame is after the operating-name change and before the puncture.
