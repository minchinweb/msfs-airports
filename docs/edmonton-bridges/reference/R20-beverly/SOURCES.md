| [Wikipedia, Clover Bar Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clover_Bar_Bridge) | Pair of Highway 16 / Yellowhead decks. Original truss completed summer 1953, named Clover Bar, connected Beverly to Strathcona County. Steel girder built just to the south in 1972, named Beverly, eastbound. Infobox: north span 1953, south span 1972. Combined 61,402 AADT (2024). Cites Edmonton Journal 18 August 2012 ("Aug. 18, 1953: Premier Manning first to drive of Clover Bar Bridge"), Aubrey, *Naming Edmonton*, p. 22, and Forth Junction for the railway figures. The railway 504 m / 42 m figures are not used here. | CC BY-SA. Text only. |

Stage 1 south side 2025, finished November 2025: deck repair, waterproofing, paving, girder, bearing and abutment repair, upgraded lighting, then three lanes for winter. Stage 2 north side April–October 2026: two lanes, 60 km/h, milling, paving, barrier replacement, girder repairs. Trail under the bridge kept open, with hoarding during overhead work. | City of Edmonton. Text used. Page photos were not downloaded. |

No Government of Alberta bridge sheet with a deck elevation or a navigation clearance was found for this deck. HistoricBridges.org has no Beverly / Clover Bar highway page in the sources checked. The City as Museum Project was searched by name and returned no measurement.
