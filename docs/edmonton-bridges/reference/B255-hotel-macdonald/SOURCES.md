# Fairmont Hotel Macdonald — sources

Photos in `photos\` are openly licensed. Each saved file is a Wikimedia thumbnail requested at a width of 1,920 px. The full-size originals were not fetched. The 8 August 2017 frame is also in `docs/edmonton-bridges/reference/F256-funicular/photos/lookout-and-truss.jpg`. A copy was saved here because the hotel roof is in that frame. It was not moved.

This folder is the hotel at 10065 100 Street. The 100 Street funicular stays in the funicular notes. The Grierson Hill Bridge stays in the grierson notes. The two Low Level river decks stay in the R13 notes. The ask spelled the hotel MacDonald. The files use the published Macdonald spelling, and they record MacDonald as the ask's spelling and as a spelling that already appears in print.
