## Tier 2 build findings (2026-10-15)

- The 2019 City orthophoto and the 2018 LiDAR clips cover the whole interchange (the clip is a rotated rectangle about 2.2 km by 1.6 km); the plan of every way in `geometry.json` sits on the photo. `elevation/README.md` was never written, so the DSM medians were sampled here (`elevation/h39-samples.json`).
- The interchange is a combination of two cloverleaf loops (west loop is the WS3 / NW chain, east loop the EN chain) with directional flyovers. WS2-10 and EN2-13 each cross **three** roadways: the Henday carriageway pair plus the Henday collector ramp on the west side (WS2: NB, SB and the SE-ramp collector; EN2: SB, NB and the NW-ramp collector). That is why they have three spans: the two piers sit in the two medians.
- Span layout check against the mapped lines: with the first pier in the median between the first two roads under and the spans from the inspections, EN3-14 (84.4 m) and WS2-10 (124.6 m) fit their OSM decks (85.8 m and 127.1 m) to within 2.5 m. The other decks are 5 to 30 m shorter than their OSM lines, so the mapped bridge ways include embankment.
- Deck tops from the 2018 first returns (fit, minus 0.12 m): N-1 691.5 m, S-6 692.1, NW-7 691.2, SE-8 693.1, WS1-9 693.8, WS2-10 701.9, WS3-11 696.3, EN1-12 697.9, EN2-13 701.5, EN3-14 691.7. Pavement under: Yellowhead 683.3 to 684.4 m under N-1 .. SE-8, 687.0 under WS3-11, 681.8 under EN3-14; Henday 689.5 under WS2-10, 690.1 under EN2-13. WS1-9 and EN1-12 cross ramps at 683.5 and 688.6 m. Those figures match the published clearances (5.5 to 5.8 m over Yellowhead, 9.0 m and 7.7 m over Henday) to within the girder depth.
- Ramps added to `geometry.json` (`ramps_added_2026_10_15`): 34 motorway / motorway_link ways, including the six ramp chains that carry the flyovers and the Yellowhead carriageways, from an OSM API map read of the box. H38 (Henday / Stony Plain Road) has no data here and was not built.
