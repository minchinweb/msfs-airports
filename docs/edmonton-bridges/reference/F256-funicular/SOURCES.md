# 100 Street Funicular — sources

Photos in `photos\` are openly licensed. Each saved file is a Wikimedia thumbnail requested at a width of 1,920 px. The full-size originals were not fetched. The 15 December 2017 frame is already in `docs/edmonton-bridges/reference/N258-grierson/photos/from-funicular.jpg`. It is cited below and was not copied.

This folder is the 100 Street funicular, the Frederick G. Todd Lookout, and the pedestrian bridge over Grierson Hill Road. The Grierson Hill Bridge stays in the grierson notes. The two Low Level river decks stay in the R13 notes.
