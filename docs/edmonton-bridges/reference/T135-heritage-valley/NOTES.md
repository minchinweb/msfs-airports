# T135 Anthony Henday LRT bridge, Heritage Valley: modeling brief

Edmonton. Capital Line South LRT crossing of Anthony Henday Drive (Alberta Highway 216), on a skew. Centre used by the project: 53.4354, -113.5237. Under construction. The newest City notice for this bridge prints no year; the brief places the arch steel delivery in June 2026. The newest dated source checked is a 22 May 2026 news article about the line, which names no figure for this bridge.

No published figure for the finished arches was found. Most structural values below are project-inventory rules from `lrt.md`, not published numbers. Those are marked as such. Nothing here was filled in by adding one citation to another. Where a figure is missing, the "Still missing" list says so.

Not this structure:

- T134 Blackmud Creek LRT bridge, 53.4409, -113.5185. Its girders were installed from 30 April 2026 (City and Ledcor announcement, reported 14 May 2026). Different crossing, different ravine.
- H29 Henday at 111 Street, 53.435, -113.514. The road bridges are over the Henday at 111 Street, with three lanes each way. The LRT bridge is a separate structure. Do not copy the road bridge's spans.
- P177 Twin Brooks, 53.4468, -113.5167. The station is on the Capital Line between T134 and T135.
- P178 Heritage Valley North, 53.42512, -113.54559. The end of Capital Line South phase 1 and the south terminus of the phase.
- H30 Whitemud Creek arch. A different structure over a creek.
- H27 and H28 at Blackmud Creek, 53.437 and -113.491 and -113.496. A different structure.
- The Valley Line West bridge over the Henday at 87 Avenue. Marigold and the City describe it. A different structure and project. Girders went in in January 2024.
- Llew Lawrence maintenance facility. A yard south of the Henday. Not a bridge.

## 1. Opening

Capital Line South is the high-floor extension from Century Park to Heritage Valley North (phase 1). The line runs north-south. T135 carries it over the Henday between Twin Brooks and Heritage Valley North. Trains run north to Century Park and the downtown, and south to Heritage Valley North (P178). The brief says a south departure toward CYEG sees this bridge.

The 2024 City booklet says the LRT "continues diagonally across Anthony Henday Drive on an LRT bridge", and that "Anthony Henday Drive traffic will have a continuous flow under the LRT bridge" (booklet, page 21). The City describes phase 1 as at street level, except for the 23 Avenue underpass and the LRT bridges over Blackmud Creek and Anthony Henday Drive (City project description, as quoted in a search summary; the page itself was not opened).

Status, from the newest sources:

- Piling north of the Henday near 111 Street, which will support the bridge (City notice, year not printed; read as a search summary, not opened; the brief gives March 2026).
- Median lane closures west of 111 Street from on or around 6 June, for about five months, for deliveries and preparatory work for the "future Anthony Henday Drive LRT bridge" (City notice; year not printed).
- Arch steel and precast girder deliveries, with single-lane closures from on or around 12 June, and erection of the girders (City notice; year not printed).

The notices print no year, so the year is taken from the brief. The City's project page (edmonton.ca/projects_plans/transit/capitalsw) was not readable on 23 November 2026 (HTTP 502). It may give a later status.

## 2. Bridge

**Type.** Two steel network arches, on a skew of 49 degrees (`lrt.md` inventory row, 14 October 2026; no published source). The 2024 City booklet shows an arch bridge in a concept rendering (page 22, "subject to change"). In that image the arch ribs look trussed or braced. The hanger pattern is not clear enough at that size to confirm "network". The booklet does not say how many arches there are, and the rendering shows arches over the deck but no dimensions.

**Spans.** Two arch spans, two concrete-girder approach spans (`lrt.md` inventory). Arch spans in metres: not published. Approach span lengths: not published. Total length: not published.

**Deck.** Post-tensioned concrete over the highway, per the `lrt.md` inventory. No source checked gives a deck type, depth, or material. The booklet does not describe the deck.

**Median pier.** One rectangular pier in the median, with chamfered corners and a hammerhead cap (`lrt.md` inventory). Piers at the end of the arch spans are that shape or a two-column bent. Pier dimensions: not published. The median notice describes deliveries and preparatory work in the median west of 111 Street. It does not mention the pier.

**Clearance under the Henday.** Not published. The `lrt.md` rule is at least 6.2 m over the mainline and ramps, "including 0.1 m tolerance and 0.2 m for a future overlay" (project inventory, undated, source not cited). Treat 6.2 m as the project's design floor. It is not a published clearance and not a deck elevation. The 0.1 m and 0.2 m allowances are also the inventory's.

**Rail and path alongside.** The booklet's 2024 plan (page 21) shows the trackway and the boulevard beside it. It does not mark a path on the bridge. Its legend lists "Proposed Visual Screen Fence" and "Safety Fence". The plan does not show where those run on the bridge. No path is confirmed on the bridge.

**Arch figures.** Arch rise: not published. Finished arch height above the deck or the ground: not published. Span-to-rise ratio: not published. The City's design-build schedule is said to tie the form to a span-rise ratio, but the appendix drawings were not readable (the schedule PDF returned HTTP 502).

## 3. Approaches

**South end, toward Heritage Valley North (P178).** The guideway runs south from the bridge through the at-grade section to P178. The mapped track length from P178 to the bridge is about 2,152 m (OpenStreetMap construction way, computed from nodes; `lrt.md` gives 2,155 m for K204). That is a plan length. It is not a climb to grade. The gradient is not published. The elevation change is not published. Where the guideway meets grade is not published.

**North end, toward Twin Brooks (P177).** The 2024 booklet (page 20) says: "South of Twin Brooks, the LRT starts to bend to the west as it travels toward Anthony Henday Drive. The LRT will gradually gain elevation in order to cross over Anthony Henday Drive." No gradient, height or length is given. Where the guideway meets grade on this side is not published. The mapped track length from the bridge to T134 is about 751 m (`lrt.md` K203: 752 m). That is a plan length, not a climb.

Ramps, retaining walls, and the approach-span lengths are not published.

## 4. Deck

**Elevation above sea level.** Not published. No datum is given. Do not convert a gauge reading.

**Elevation above the road.** Not published. The 6.2 m figure is a clearance rule, not a deck elevation.

**Deck thickness and depth.** Not published.

**Track centres.** Not published. The booklet shows one trackway in the plan (page 21), which is one line, not a count of tracks.

**Width.** Not published.

**Noise or safety screens.** The legend lists a "Proposed Visual Screen Fence" and a "Safety Fence". No screen height, length, or location on the bridge is published.
