## Not used, and why

- The PCI 2005 paper on the 111 Street bridge over the Henday (Afhami and Ramsay), and the 111 Street bridge spans of 55 and 51 m from a search summary: the road bridge (H29), not the LRT bridge.
- A Whitemud Creek arch of 40 m span and 7.5 m rise: H30, a different structure.
- The 2024 City booklet pages 17 and 23 (Blackmud Creek and Anthony Henday Drive/MacEwan): different structures.
