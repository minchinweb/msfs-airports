# Y69 street photos: sources

These are Mapillary street-level frames, taken from a vehicle. They show what a driver sees on the approaches and the Yellowhead service road. They are not reference for the finished deck.

## Licence

The Mapillary API query on 2026-11-23 returned no licence field for these images. Mapillary's default licence, CC BY-SA 4.0, is recorded here as unconfirmed. Creator usernames are kept for attribution. Panoramas are excluded. The images are not scaled.

## Query

- Endpoint: graph.mapillary.com/images, bbox -113.5600,53.5785,-113.5520,53.5840 (about 0.008 by 0.0055 degrees, within the size limit).
- Query returned 187 images. 13 lie within 150 m of the Alberta record point 53.580893, -113.556664. Three qualify under the 30 m separation rule. A fourth was not 30 m from the others.
- Distances are from the record point. Captured times are UTC.
