# Rossdale Road over McDougall Hill — sources

Photos in `photos\` are openly licensed. The one saved file is a 1,920 px Wikimedia thumbnail. The full-size original was not fetched. The original is 3,072 px wide. A file that was useful but not openly licensed is listed under "Not downloaded" with the URL only.

This folder is the road span where Rossdale Road crosses McDougall Hill Road, on the north bank, west of the Low Level north end. The Grierson Hill Bridge stays in the Grierson notes. The two Low Level river decks stay in the R13 notes. The 100 Street funicular stays in the funicular notes.
