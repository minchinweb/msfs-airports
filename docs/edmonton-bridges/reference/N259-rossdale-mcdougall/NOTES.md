# N259 Rossdale Road over McDougall Hill — modeling brief

Edmonton. A short road span on the north bank, west of the Low Level north end. Centre used here: 53.537724, -113.493132. That point is the midpoint along OpenStreetMap way 37120045, half the horizontal length of the coordinates read on 14 October 2026. It is not a surveyed centre. The road on the deck is Rossdale Road NW. The way is one-way, asphalt, tagged 50 km/h. It has no lanes tag.

This is not N258, the Grierson Hill Bridge. That span is way 106711211, centre 53.538564, -113.488789. It carries Grierson Hill Road over McDougall Hill Road. The deck there was replaced in 2012. This span is a different way. The horizontal distance between the two midpoints is 302.2 m. That distance is not a clearance, and it is not a structure length.

This is not one of the two Low Level river decks. The 1900 northbound deck is way 31882107. The 1948/1949 southbound deck is way 22916473. Those are the through trusses across the North Saskatchewan River. McDougall Hill Road runs onto those decks. This span does not cross the river. The code is N259. It is not an R code. It is not N258.

It carries Rossdale Road over McDougall Hill Road. Alberco lists Rossdale Rd - McDougall Overpass Rehabilitation as its own project, on a separate page from Grierson Bridge Rehabilitation. The mapped centreline crosses McDougall Hill Road and does not share a node with that way. It is not a culvert. The mapped line is 49.1 m. At 3,500 ft AGL that deck does not read as a river bridge. At 1,000 ft the grade separation does read, because the two roads are not at one level. No height was read from a photograph, and none was made by subtracting one citation from another.

Numbers below are cited. Where two sources disagree, both are given. A blank in the "Still missing" list has no published figure in the sources checked. Nothing here was filled in by adding one citation to another. OpenStreetMap `maxheight` tags are mapper tags. They are not deck elevations. This way has no `maxheight` tag and no `width` tag.

## Bridge

**What it crosses.** McDougall Hill Road. Way 37120045 is tagged `bridge=yes` and `layer=1`. Its centreline crosses way 636183922 in plan. The two ways do not share a node. Way 636183922 is McDougall Hill Road NW, two lanes, one-way, asphalt, 50 km/h, 165.5 m. It is not bridge-tagged. Node order on that way runs from the west side of the crossing to the east end. The one-way follows that order. The east end is also the end of Rossdale Road way 113653301, which does not use this deck. This span does not cross the river, a rail line, or a creek in the sources checked.

**Year.** A year for the overpass was not found. Alberco's page gives a completion date of June 2013. The contract value on that page is $1,400,000. They were not averaged. The $1,400,000 figure is Alberco's contract value. It is not the $4.5 million deck replacement of the Grierson Hill Bridge.

The Montgomery line has no year. It was not used as the year built, and it was not used as the year of the June 2013 work.

## What reads from the air

Include it in the Low Level north-bank scene. The deck is the grade separation where westbound Rossdale Road crosses McDougall Hill Road, on the same bank as the Low Level north end, 302.2 m from the Grierson Hill Bridge midpoint. At 1,000 ft the two roads are not at one level. At 3,500 ft the 49.1 m deck does not read as a river bridge. A model that joins them at one grade is the wrong interchange.

If that choice is wrong, a later pass would have drawn a deck that does not show at 3,500 ft, and would have missed leaving westbound Rossdale Road and McDougall Hill Road at one grade.

The 6 September 2008 photograph is the open view from the west. The camera position on the file is 53.537926, -113.496607, 231.0 m west of this midpoint. Downtown towers fill the frame. A concrete span crosses a lower road in the lower right, with a curved road beside it. The Low Level truss is not in the frame. The Grierson midpoint is 522.1 m from this camera.
