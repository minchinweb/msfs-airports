# G117 102 Avenue at Groat Road — sources

Photos in `photos\` are CC BY-SA 2.0. All three saved files are 1,920 px Wikimedia thumbnails. The full-size originals were not fetched. They show the deck and the railing on 9 September 2016, after the 15 July 2016 reopening and while cones were still on the bridge. None is a side elevation. Groat Road, the river span, and Wellington Bridge are not in the frames.
