# R11 Walterdale Bridge — sources

Photos in `photos\` are openly licensed. A file that was useful but not openly licensed is listed under "Not downloaded" with the URL only. Six of the saved files are 1,920 px Wikimedia thumbnails: the full-size originals were refused with HTTP 429. The other four are the full-size files.

This folder is the 2017 arch. Photographs of the 1913 truss were not saved.

| [City of Edmonton, Walterdale Bridge](https://www.edmonton.ca/projects_plans/walterdale-bridge) | Two 56 m tall arches. Thrust blocks on both banks, no permanent in-river piers. Three northbound lanes. Traffic: two lanes on 18 September 2017; all three lanes and the west sidewalk on 29 September 2017; east shared-use path later that fall. Grand opening September 2018. Construction history on the same page: 32 deck hangers in place by mid-September 2016; 14 hangers stated for the shared-use path; path up to 9 m wide at the ends and 4.2 m in the centre; second arch lift raised to 54 m above the river. | City of Edmonton. Text used. Page photos and videos were not downloaded. |
| [City features page, archived 6 July 2014](https://web.archive.org/web/20140706213241/http://www.edmonton.ca/transportation/road_projects/walterdale-bridge-features.aspx) | Two 56 m high arches, span 206 m, thrust blocks on both banks, no permanent in-river piers. Each thrust block about 600 m³ of concrete, 20 m below the road elevation. Sidewalk on the west, separated shared-use path on the east. Abutments on drilled piles. Queen Elizabeth Park Road and Walterdale Hill Road closed in turn for re-grading. | City of Edmonton, via the Internet Archive. Wikipedia's 56 m figure cites this page. |
| [City 2013 yearbook (PDF)](https://www.edmonton.ca/sites/default/files/public-files/assets/PDF/Walterdale_Bridge_Replacement_2013_Yearbook.pdf) | Two 56 m tall arches. Arches 206 m long, rising 56 m above the thrust blocks. Walkway on the west, separated shared-use path on the east. Thrust blocks printed as "10 m 2", founded 20 m below the road elevation. Abutments of drilled piles and retaining walls. Diagram labels: arch height 43 m, length 206 m, height above water at mid-span 13.3 m. Three vehicle lanes. | City of Edmonton. The 13.3 m label does not say whether it is to the roadway or the soffit, and it does not name a water datum. |
| [City project update, August 2014 (PDF)](https://www.edmonton.ca/sites/default/files/public-files/assets/WalterdaleBridge_ProjectUpdate_Aug2014.pdf) | Four thrust blocks. When finished, 10 m across and 20 m below the road elevation. Concrete slope set at 45 degrees. | City of Edmonton. |
| [City project update, October 2015 (PDF)](https://www.edmonton.ca/sites/default/files/public-files/assets/WalterdaleBridge_ProjectUpdate_Oct2015.pdf) | Completed arches will span 206 m and be 54 m tall. Four thrust blocks, each 10 m across, buried 20 m below the road. | City of Edmonton. This 54 m is a construction-period figure. The City's later project page says 56 m. |
| [Spencer Environmental, site location study, October 2012 (PDF)](https://pub-edmonton.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=36992) | Planning alignment, before detailed design. Bridge spanning approximately 240 m, at a 15° angle, skewing eastward, east of the old bridge, single span, no instream piers. North end of the alignment at 105 Street and River Valley Road, then a slight realignment of 105 Street. South of the river the alignment crosses part of Dantzer's Hill and forks into Walterdale Hill Road and Queen Elizabeth Park Road, each realigned approximately 300 m. | City of Edmonton council file, prepared for ISL. The 240 m and 15° were not repeated as as-built figures in the design papers below. The 300 m lengths are lengths of roadwork in that study, not measured climbs. |
| [Montgomery and others, "Walterdale Bridge Replacement", 2014 (PDF)](https://www.aisc.org/media/niomyn3k/montgomery-2014-wsbs-final.pdf) | Design paper. Through-arch, 206 m bank to bank, deck 230 m between abutment centrelines. Three northbound lanes, wide sidewalk west, separate shared-use path east. Thrust arch chosen over a tied arch. Arch ribs box sections, about 2.5 m by 2.5 m at the base to 1.4 m by 1.4 m at the crown. Plate 100 mm at the base to 75 mm at the crown. Ribs inclined 13.5° from vertical. Each rib spliced at 20 places. Shared-use path clear width 8 m at the banks and 4.2 m near mid-span. Delta piers adjacent to the water. Hangers are parallel-strand cables in HDPE pipe, fixed at the upper fork, adjustable at the lower. Excavations for the thrust blocks about 18 m deep, plan about 15.5 m by 14.2 m. | Paper hosted by AISC. Text used. The drawings in the PDF were not extracted. |
| [Canadian Consulting Engineer award submission, 2019 (PDF)](https://www.canadianconsultingengineer.com/awards/pdfs/2019/B-06_DIALOGWalterdale.pdf) | As-built account from the design team. Single span, 206 m bank to bank, 230 m between abutment centrelines. Arch ribs rise 40 m above the roadway. Box size and plate thickness as in the 2014 paper. Sidewalk west, shared-use path east. Shared-use path girder 1.4 m deep, clear width 8 m at the banks and 4.2 m near mid-span, on abutments, on delta piers near the water, and on hangers from the east arch. Thrust-block footing 9 m by 10.5 m, 44 micropiles of 200 mm diameter embedded 18 m, excavation 18 m deep and about 16 m by 14 m. Support leg above grade stressed to the rib with 58-65 mm diameter bars. Each rib is 21 segments, numbered from the south support leg. Temporary tension ties were removed after the lifts. No bridge piers in the river. Walterdale Hill narrowed from three lanes to two from 109 Street northward. Paths beyond the plazas slope less than 5%. Structure designed so a future fourth lane can go on the west side. | Canadian Consulting Engineer awards PDF. Text used. Drawings were not extracted. "58-65 mm diameter" is quoted as printed; it is not clear whether that is 58 bars of 65 mm or a range of bar diameters. |
| [Canadian Consulting Engineer, 22 August 2013](https://www.canadianconsultingengineer.com/new-walterdale-bridge-in-edmonton-has-unique-design/) | Kris Lima, then DIALOG's construction manager, interviewed during construction. Deck 230 m, single span, no in-stream piers. Arch extends 43 m above the deck or about 55 m above normal river level. Ribs taper from about 2.5 m at the base to about 1.4 m at the top, inclined about 14° off vertical. Shared-use path on the east, gap from the road deck ranging from 8 m to 2 m. West side is a straight bike and foot way, which could later be removed for another vehicle lane. Thrust blocks described as 400 m³, 15 m below grade. Arch foundations 12 m from the abutments. | Magazine article. The 400 m³ and 15 m disagree with the later City and award figures. Both are kept in `NOTES.md`. |
| [Pedelta, New Walterdale Bridge](https://www.pedelta.com/new-walterdale-bridge-p-66-en) | Erection engineer for the decks and hangers. Deck 230 m. Two 206 m thrust arches, 54 m tall. Traffic deck 22.4 m wide: three northbound lanes, two shoulders, barriers, and a sidewalk. Shared-use path on the east, walkway on the west. Two planes of 16 hangers at 10 m centres, and one plane of 16 hangers on the shared-use path girder. | Pedelta project page. The 54 m and the 16 shared-use-path hangers disagree with other sources. Both are kept. |
| [DIALOG, Walterdale Bridge](https://dialogdesign.ca/projects/walterdale-bridge/) | Designer. Single span, 206 m bank to bank, arches 56 m tall, 42 steel segments, heaviest segment 125 tonnes, 1,500 bolts at each connection. Deck on 32 hangers. Sidewalk west, separated shared-use path east. Thrust blocks about 600 m³ and 44 micropiles, 20 m below the road elevation. Three lanes now, a fourth lane designed for. Flood lines marked on the abutments; fewer on the north side because that bank is higher. Roads realigned. No new approach length or gradient. | DIALOG. Text used. Photographs and renders on the page were not downloaded. |
| [ACCIONA, project article](https://www.acciona.com/updates/articles/walterdale-bridge-major-engineering-feat-enhances-city) | Contractor. Length 230 m abutment to abutment, span 206 m, deck 22.5 m wide. The same article says the arch was lifted to 54 m above the river and, separately, that the arch is 54 m measured from the deck. Thrust blocks 20 m underground, 600 m³ of concrete and 100 tons of steel each. | ACCIONA. The two 54 m statements in this article are not the same measurement. Photographs were not downloaded. |
| [ACCIONA Australia, project page](https://www.acciona.com.au/projects/walterdale-bridge) | Span 230 m between abutment axes. Deck 22.40 m wide. Arch rises 43 m above the deck and 55 m above the river. This page puts the pedestrian walkway on the east of the 22.4 m deck and the shared-use footbridge on the west, separated by 8 m to 2 m. Ribs from 2.50 m at the base to 1.40 m at the crown. | ACCIONA. The east and west arrangement on this page is the reverse of the City, DIALOG, and the award submission. The designer's arrangement is the one used in `NOTES.md`. |
| [Wikipedia, Walterdale Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walterdale_Bridge) | Through arch. Total length 230 m, citing an older DIALOG URL. Longest span 206 m, citing Pedelta. Opened 29 September 2017. Arches 56 m tall, citing the archived City features page. Three northbound lanes. Thrust blocks on the banks, no piers. | CC BY-SA. Text only. The infobox photograph was not saved; the elevations below are clearer. The 1913 truss infobox was not used. |
