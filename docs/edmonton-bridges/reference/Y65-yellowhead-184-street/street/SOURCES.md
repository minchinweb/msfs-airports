# Y65 street photos: sources

Mapillary images, gathered 2026-11-23. They show what a driver sees near the structure. They are not views of the finished deck.

## Licence

The Mapillary API did not return a licence for these images. Mapillary's default is CC BY-SA 4.0. That is unconfirmed here. Creator usernames are kept for attribution. Images are not scaled. The thumbnails are Mapillary's 1024 px versions.

## Query

Bounding box: min lon -113.6430, min lat 53.5688, max lon -113.6362, max lat 53.5755. That covers the Y65 deck and approaches plus about 150 m. The query returned 39 images. None was a panorama. Four were taken nearest the deck, at least 30 m apart.

All four are from one dashcam sequence (BlackVue DR900M, overlay speed about 89 to 90 km/h). They were taken from a vehicle on the freeway. Distance is measured from each image's position to the nearest OSM deck line, not from what the image shows.

## Which images show the structure

None. All four show the Yellowhead carriageway. The 184 Street deck, its piers and its approach ramps are not in frame in any of them.

The other 35 images in the query were not saved. Two of them were captured on 2014-08-12, south of the deck. They were not picked because the four nearest images came first.

## Gaps

No image of the 184 Street deck, its piers, or its underside was found in the Mapillary query. A wider query may find one.
