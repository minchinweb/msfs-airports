# Y65 Yellowhead / 184 Street: modeling brief

Edmonton. A two-span concrete-deck bridge that carries 184 Street NW over Yellowhead Trail (Alberta Highway 16). Alberta records it as in service from 2004. Centre used by the project: 53.573, -113.639 (lat, lon). The Alberta point for the structure is 53.572236, -113.639383, about 89 m from that centre. The OpenStreetMap plot is 53.5727, -113.6392.

184 Street runs north and south over the freeway. Yellowhead runs east and west underneath, in both carriageways (Alberta: Highway 16, control section 17, R1 at 0.784 km and L1 at 0.790 km).

This is not Y66, Yellowhead / 178 Street (53.572, -113.631). Y66 is the next exchange east. Do not reuse its numbers.

It is not the CNR overpass at 118 Avenue. That is a separate Alberta file (85131, structure name "184 STREET - CNR (118 AVENUE) (B294)"). It carries 184 Street over the railway about 300 m south of this bridge. This brief covers Alberta file 85130, "184 STREET - YELLOWHEAD TRAIL (B291)".

Numbers below are cited. Tags in brackets: [AB] is the Alberta Structure-Export record, [OSM] is bridge-geometry.json, [CTV] and [City] are the 2026 news and City items. A blank has no published figure in the sources checked.

## Bridge

**Type.** The Alberta record gives span type code WG, a concrete deck (PCONC), and a rectangular pier shaft (RECTANGLE) [AB]. The record does not expand WG. It does not say girder count, girder depth, or whether the spans are steel or concrete. Structure type is not published in the sources checked.

**Spans.** Two spans, 45 m and 42 m [AB]. Maximum span 45 m [AB].

**Length.** Nominal bridge length 87 m [AB]. The two OSM deck ways are 87.4 m and 88.1 m long in plan [OSM]. That agrees with 87 m. The length between abutment walls is not published as a separate figure.

**Girder or truss depth.** Not published.

**Piers.** Two spans imply one interior support. The record does not give a pier count [AB]. Pier cap and shaft are concrete, the shaft is rectangular [AB]. Pier height and footing size are not published. Abutment type is not published.

**Clearance.** Not published. Vertical clearance under the deck is not in the Alberta record or in any source found.

**Sidewalks.** Left sidewalk width 2.5 m [AB]. The record stores it as 2500, which reads as millimetres. It does not say which side is left. The OSM extract has a cycleway on the deck (way 234183148, 101.0 m in plan). It lies outside the southbound deck line, about 7.6 m from it in plan. It is not confirmed as the left sidewalk.

**Build and rehabilitation.** The Alberta record shows a new structure in 2004. The status date is 2004-07-05 [AB]. The total build cost in the record is 4,500,000 [AB]. Its currency and year are not labelled. A search summary of the 2026 CTV closure story says the work was the first renovation since the bridge opened in the early 2000s. The article body did not load. The 2026 works extended service life and improved safety for all users, per the City summary in search results (the City page itself did not load). No change to the silhouette is published.

## Approaches

Each end is split by direction. The OSM ways are one-way lines [OSM].

**South end.** Northbound onto the deck, 207.4 m plan length, from the south edge of the extract at 53.570495. Southbound off the deck, 221.8 m plan length, to the south edge of the extract at 53.570385. Both continue south in the wider road network. Gradient: not published. Elevation change: not published. Where they meet grade: not published.

**North end.** Northbound off the deck, 67.5 m plan length. Southbound onto the deck, 425.6 m plan length, from the north edge of the extract at 53.576939. Gradient: not published. Elevation change: not published. Where they meet grade: not published.

**Interchange ramps.** Two motorway_link ways (730.3 m and 470.0 m plan length) join the 184 Street approaches to Yellowhead. They are not approaches to this bridge, and they are not climb data.

**Junction type.** Wikipedia calls the 184 Street junction a partial cloverleaf (exit 379) and says it opened in 2004. It cites Alberta Transportation's TIMS report for the date. Wikipedia's Yellowhead Trail article says Yellowhead "passes underneath 184 Street and over the Canadian National Railway". That contradicts [AB] and the City page title, which both put 184 Street over Yellowhead. The Alberta record, the City title and the OSM geometry all agree. The Wikipedia wording is kept here as a disagreement, not used.

## Deck

**Elevation above sea level.** Not published. The datum is not published.

**Elevation above the road underneath.** Not published. [AB] gives kilometre points on Highway 16, not heights.

**Thickness.** Not published. The wearing surface is polymer-modified asphalt (ACP PM) [AB]. Its depth is not given.

**Width.** The gross deck width is 27.2 m. The total clear roadway is 18.8 m [AB]. The width of each carriageway is not published. In the OSM plot the two one-way deck lines are about 12.7 m apart at their midpoints [OSM]. That is a plan spacing of two mapped centrelines, not a carriageway width.

**Lanes.** OSM tags each one-way deck way lanes=2 [OSM]. That is a mapper tag. It is not a published lane count.

**Sidewalk or path.** See the Bridge section.

**Crossfall and longitudinal grade.** Not published.

**Speed.** OSM tags maxspeed 60 km/h on the approaches [OSM]. That is a mapper tag.

**Estimated traffic.** The record gives 19,300 vehicles a day, estimated for 2014 [AB].
