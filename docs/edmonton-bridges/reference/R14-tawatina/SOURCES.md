# R14 Tawatinâ Bridge — sources

Photos in `photos\` are openly licensed. Eight are the original files. `elevation-and-south-girder-grierson-hill.jpg` is a 2,560 px thumbnail because the original is 8,021 × 5,347 and about 60 MB. A useful picture that is not openly licensed is listed under "Not downloaded" with the URL only.

Low Level, James MacDonald, and Dawson are not in this folder.

| [Project agreement, Schedule 5 Part 2, Sustainable Urban Integration, 8 February 2016 (PDF)](https://www.edmonton.ca/sites/default/files/public-files/Project-Agreement-Valley-Line-LRT-Schedule-5-Part-2-Sustainable-Urban-Integration.pdf) | City of Edmonton requirements, not a survey of the finished bridge. Section 2-11.5: extradosed bridge; two pylons on the North River Pier; no member connecting the pylons above the deck; south river pier the same shape only up to the superstructure; path through each river pier; pier base at most 8.5 m wide above the pile cap; pylon height measured from the top of deck, minimum 24 m and maximum 30 m; pylon width at the deck at most 4 m in elevation; taper at most 1 in 60. Five to ten cables each side of each pylon. Box either constant depth at most 3.5 m, or variable depth at most 4.0 m at the river piers and at most 3.0 m at midspan. Path hung on steel hangers, functional width at least 7.8 m, centre at least 3.0 m, wood sides at least 1.8 m. Lookouts at least 20 m from the pier face, seating at least 7 m². South guideway and 98 Avenue bridge are a separate section: constant depth to the ramp immediately north of Muttart stop; a through girder allowed over 98 Avenue; park piers at least 35 m apart; span just north of 98 Avenue at least 30 m. | City of Edmonton. | [Valley Line Stage 1 environmental assessment, 2013 (PDF)](https://www.edmonton.ca/sites/default/files/public-files/assets/RoadsTraffic/Attachment1_ValleyLine-Stage1_EnvironmentalImpactScreeningAssessment_21-57.pdf) | Reference design, which the report says to treat as approximate. Muttart approach on a steep grade; five retaining walls then conceived, 120–230 m long and 2.5–6 m high. The elevation drawing in the report labels river spans 100 m, 110 m, and 50 m, three further spans of 40 m marked approximate, and a grade of −1.90%. The 98 Avenue drawing labels −5.600% and −0.275%. A second river-profile grade was not legible. | City of Edmonton. Drawing labels are figure text. The 40 m spans and the grades are reference design. |

Box 3.5 m tall by 11 m wide. Path 8 m wide, steel, timber, and asphalt, hung on steel rods. Segment lengths as in `NOTES.md`. North abutment, Pier 2, and the end of span 3 on guided pot bearings. Load-model lanes 3,420 mm wide, offset 2,250 mm from deck centre. Deck modeled flat, without plinths. No pylon height, no deck elevation, no crossfall. | University of Alberta thesis. Text used. Drawings in the PDF were not extracted. |
