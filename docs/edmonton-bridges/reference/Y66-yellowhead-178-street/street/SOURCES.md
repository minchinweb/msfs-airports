# Y66 street photos: sources

Mapillary images, gathered 2026-11-23. They show what a driver sees on the Yellowhead freeway near the structure. They are not views of the finished deck.

## Licence

The Mapillary API did not return a licence for these images. Mapillary's default is CC BY-SA 4.0. That is unconfirmed here. Creator usernames are kept for attribution. Images are not scaled. The thumbnails are Mapillary's 1024 px versions.

## Query

Bounding box: min lon -113.6457, min lat 53.5693, max lon -113.6239, max lat 53.5756. That covers the Y66 mapped geometry plus about 150 m. The query was split into two tiles, and the tiles returned 117 images in total. None was a panorama, and each had a thumbnail. Four were taken nearest the mapped Y66 lines, at least 30 m apart.

All four are from one dashcam sequence (BlackVue DR900M, overlay speed 82 to 89 km/h), taken from a vehicle on the freeway. The distance is measured from each image's position to the nearest mapped Y66 way. It is not what the image shows. The same sequence supplied the Y65 images.

## Which images show the structure

None clearly shows the 178 Street crossing or the bridge. `mly-608046484139354.jpg` shows a bridge deck in the distance ahead on the freeway. It is not identified as this structure, and it may be the CNR overpass. The other three show the freeway only.

## Gaps

- The query returned 117 images. Only four were saved, chosen by distance from the mapped lines, not by whether they show the structure.
- No saved image shows a 178 Street approach or cross street.
- All four saved images were viewed. The descriptions in the table come from those views.
