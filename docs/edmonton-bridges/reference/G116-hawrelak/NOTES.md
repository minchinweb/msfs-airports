# G116 Hawrelak access — modeling brief

Emily Murphy Park Road over Groat Road, south of the river, between Hawrelak Park and the university. Centre used by the project: 53.5322, -113.5383.

## What it is

This is the Mayfair Bridge. Graham's project page calls it the Emily Murphy Park Bridge over Groat Road. Al-Terra calls it the Emily Murphy Park Road Bridge over Groat Road. The Edmonton Journal (Elise Stolte, 7 March 2018) calls it the Mayfair Bridge over Groat Road near Emily Murphy Park, south of the river, rehabilitated in 2018. The Journal and Global News on 20 April 2018 name the same bridge, south of the river. The Journal of Commerce on 18 April 2018 calls it the Emily Murphy Park Road Bridge south of the river.

It is not the river span. The river span is R8. Do not reuse that span's lengths, the east shared-use path width, or the pier notes. The Journal of Commerce puts "approximately 300 metres" and 1955 on the river bridge. Those figures are not this deck.

It is not the Groat Road bridge over Victoria Park Road. That span is at the north end of the river bridge.

## Bridge

**Type.** Grade separation, rehabilitated, not replaced. Alberta Transportation's structures list, file 76535, names it MAYFAIR BRG, MAYFAIR BRIDGE ON GROAT ROAD, and prints year 1966. The row prints PJ MAJOR BRIDGE GS 3. On the same report the Groat river row prints RV 7, and that bridge has seven spans, and file B408 prints GF 1 for a one-span bridge. Read the 3 as three spans on that pattern. The other numbers on the row were not assigned. The PDF interleaves columns.

**Work that shows.** Graham's project page says this bridge was a single-season rehabilitation in the 2018–2020 contract. The Journal of 7 March 2018 says 2018. Al-Terra lists completion in 2021 and says Al-Terra was a subconsultant to DIALOG. What the rehabilitation changed on the girders, the railings, or the piers is not described.

**Spans.** A published span length was not found. The mapped deck is the park road plus the ramps, all tagged bridge and layer 1. Use those lines for the plan. Do not add them into a structure length.
