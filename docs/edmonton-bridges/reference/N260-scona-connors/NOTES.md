# N260 Low Level south roadworks — modeling brief

Edmonton. The road span where Scona Road crosses Connors Road, on the south slope above the Low Level Bridge. Centre used here: 53.533941, -113.483610, the midpoint of OpenStreetMap way 22959547. The mapped way is two lanes, one-way, asphalt, tagged 50 km/h.

This is not a Low Level deck. The 1900 northbound deck is way 31882107. The 1948/1949 southbound deck is way 22916473. Those are the through trusses across the North Saskatchewan River. This span does not cross the river. The south-bank roadworks, including this span, the 98 Avenue spans, and Connors Road over the ramp, are N260. Scona Road Bridge (B065) is the span to omit. N260 is not an R code.

It is not the James MacDonald river girder. Those decks are ways 25455305 and 25455308. They stay in the R12 geometry.

The ask called it a bridge on Scona Drive over Conner's Road, at the cloverleaf on the south side of the hill. The City name is Scona Road Bridge, B065, Scona Road over Connors Road.

Numbers below are cited. Where two sources disagree, both are given. A blank in the "Still missing" list has no published figure in the sources checked. Nothing here was filled in by adding one citation to another. OpenStreetMap `maxheight` tags are mapper tags. They are not deck elevations. Way 22959547 has no `maxheight` tag.

## Name

The City's bridge-renewal inventory lists Scona Road Bridge (B065). The year-built column is 1957. The bridge-renewal update names the work as rehabilitation of Scona Road Bridge over Connors Road. A tender listing uses B065 Scona Road over Connors Road. Those three agree on the road names and on which road is overhead.

The May 2026 Low Level report says the trusses connect Connors Road and Scona Road on the south side of the river with Rossdale Road and McDougall Hill Road on the north. That sentence is the river crossing. The project list uses the same pair of names for the Low Level south end. Neither sentence is a length for this span.

Wikipedia's street list says 99 Street becomes Scona Road at Saskatchewan Drive, descends into the valley, and intersects Connors Road and 98 Avenue at an interchange. Wikipedia's avenue list says Connors Road begins at Scona Road and 98 Avenue, just south of the Low Level Bridge or east of the James MacDonald Bridge. OpenStreetMap names the ways Scona Road NW and Connors Road NW. The NW is the quadrant suffix. It is not a second road.

The ask said Scona Drive, and Conner's Road with an apostrophe. No City source checked uses Scona Drive for this road, or an apostrophe in Connors. The mapper tag `destination:street=South Scona Road` is on way 106711220, the one-lane ramp off the James MacDonald eastbound deck. That ramp is already in the R12 geometry. The tag is not the City bridge name.

Wikipedia also says Connors Road and 83 Street used to be signed as Highway 14A, and that the designation was phased out in the 1970s. That is a former highway number. It is not the present signed name, and it is not the year of this deck.

## Bridge

**What it crosses.** Connors Road. Way 22959547 is tagged `bridge=yes` and `layer=1`. Its centreline crosses way 34388889 in plan. The two ways do not share a node. Way 34388889 is Connors Road NW, one-way, and it is not bridge-tagged. Node order on 34388889 is toward the east, so the Connors traffic under the span is eastbound, away from the Low Level. Node order on 22959547 is from the south end, the uphill end, to the north, toward the river, so the Scona traffic on the span is down the hill.

**Year.** 1957, the year-built column for B065. The same row gives an anticipated construction start year of 2025 and calls the scope a minor rehabilitation, to be coordinated with northbound Low Level Bridge construction. The tender listing for that rehabilitation, which also names B126, gives a start date of 15 January 2024 and a contract value of $2,156,465. The 2024 date and the 2025 year are both published. They were not averaged. The dollar figure is the listing's value for the contract that names both bridges. It was not split. The May 2026 report puts the northbound Low Level rehabilitation in 2027. Whether the minor rehabilitation of this span was built, and what it changed, was not found.

**Spans.** Not published. The mapped centreline is one straight segment between two nodes. That is the mapped line. It is not a span count.

**Length.** No published structure length was found. The horizontal length of way 22959547 is 50.4 m. That is the OpenStreetMap centreline. It is not a published structure length.

**Type.** Not published for this span. Way 22959547 has no `bridge:structure` tag. The concrete decks in the photographs from the north are the 98 Avenue spans, described below. They were not used as the type of this deck.

**Piers.** Not published. A count was not made from a photograph. No photograph found isolates the soffit of way 22959547.

**Clearance under the deck.** Not published. Way 22959547 has no `maxheight` tag. Way 34388889, the Connors way under it, has no `maxheight` tag. Connors way 697622884, under the eastbound 98 Avenue bridges, is tagged `maxheight=4.3`. Connors ways 22917297 and 31883773, under the westbound 98 Avenue bridge, are tagged `maxheight=4.6`. Connors way 112328116, east of way 34388889, is tagged `maxheight=6.1`. No published source found states 4.3 m, 4.6 m, or 6.1 m. None of those tags is on the road under this span. None of them is a deck elevation.

**Elevation.** Not published for the deck, the soffit, or the road underneath. The vertical datum is missing.

## The other spans in the interchange

The south bank has more than this one overpass. The river decks are not part of it.

**98 Avenue, at the valley floor.** These spans sit immediately east of the James MacDonald abutment. They are not the river girder.

The City inventory's row 19 is 98 Avenue Eastbound Bridge over Scona Road (B126). The year-built column is 1971. The anticipated construction start year is 2025, and the scope is a minor rehabilitation, coordinated with the northbound Low Level work. The tender listing calls the same number B126 98 Ave Eastbound over Connors Road. The Strathcona Community League page, fetched on 14 October 2026, says 98 Avenue Bridge (over Connors Road), and the City project URL on that page returned 404. The inventory says the road underneath is Scona Road. The tender listing and the league page say Connors Road. Both wordings are published. They were not averaged.

On the map, eastbound way 106711204 is 98 Avenue NW, two lanes, one-way, bridge-tagged, 81.1 m. It crosses Connors Road way 697622884. It does not cross a way named Scona Road. An unnamed two-lane ramp, way 106711230, is also bridge-tagged, 81.3 m, and it crosses that same Connors way. Westbound way 22916470 is 98 Avenue NW, three lanes, bridge-tagged, width tag 11.1, 76.3 m. It crosses Connors Road ways 22917297 and 31883773. No City number was found for the westbound span. The inventory row says eastbound. The 1971 was not copied onto way 22916470. No published source found states the 11.1 m tag. The R12 notes already record that the same tag on the river decks is not a published width.

The photographs from the north show this valley-floor concrete structure. They are described in the air section. No structure name beyond those photographs was found. No girder or slab designation was published for B126.

The short 98 Avenue ways between the river decks and these spans stay in the R12 geometry. Westbound, way 926792878 joins way 22916470 to the river deck. Eastbound, way 106711215 leaves the river deck and splits into way 926792877, which joins way 106711204, and way 926792876, which joins way 106711230.

Way 106711220 is the one-lane ramp off the eastbound river deck. The live tag is `destination:street=South Scona Road`. The ramp ends on southbound Scona Road at way 472624621. It belongs to James MacDonald. It is already in the R12 geometry. It was not copied here. It is not a second river span, and it is not way 22959547.

**Connors Road over a ramp.** Way 34388891 is Connors Road NW, bridge-tagged, 42.7 m. It has no lanes tag. It crosses ramp way 106711213. It does not cross Scona Road. No City number was found. It is not B065, and it is not B126. The midpoint is 77.9 m from the midpoint of way 22959547. That figure is a horizontal distance. It is not a clearance. Westbound Connors uses this span. Eastbound Connors is way 34388889, the road under the Scona span, and it does not use way 34388891.

**Not these spans.** Way 25364865 is the Kâhasinîskâk Bridge, the pedestrian span over Connors Road further east. It is not a road span of this interchange. Its geometry was not copied.

Ways 106711192, 106711212, and 1223720740 are the one-lane links at the Low Level south end. They stay in the R13 geometry. They join the Connors ways at the truss. They are not the Scona Road span.

Way 447942631 is a bridge-tagged footway beside the westbound 98 Avenue bridge. Way 302605460 is a bridge-tagged footway crossing ramp 106711213. Neither was copied as a road deck.

The July 2026 Low Level bulletin names Connors Road and Scona Road. Its cloverleaf sentence is the connections between Grierson Hill and McDougall Hill. That is the north bank. It is not this interchange.

## What reads from the air

Omit Scona Road over Connors Road. The span is on the south slope. Its midpoint is 404.2 m, horizontally, from the south end of the southbound Low Level deck. The 30 May 2007 photograph and the 7 April 2008 photograph are from the north, the runway 30 side of the valley. Both show a concrete overpass in the open, immediately beyond the truss, with roads under it and beside it. That overpass is the 98 Avenue span. The midpoint of westbound way 22916470 is 138.1 m from the same truss node. The hill behind that span is trees. In the April frame the trees are bare, and the road lines on the slope can be seen. Neither frame isolates a soffit for way 22959547. The 10 February 2025 photograph, from the south slope, shows a concrete span below the hill with trees in front of it. The truss is not in that frame, and the span was not identified as way 22959547. No height was read from any of the three.

Model the 98 Avenue spans with the Low Level south end. They are the concrete overpass in the photographs from the north.

If omitting the Scona span is wrong, a later pass would miss northbound Scona Road crossing over eastbound Connors Road, and the one-lane loops between the two Scona carriageways. Those roads would be drawn at one grade on the slope.
