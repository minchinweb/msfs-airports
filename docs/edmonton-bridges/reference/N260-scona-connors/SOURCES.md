# Scona Road Bridge — sources

Photos in `photos\` are openly licensed. All three saved files are 1,920 px Wikimedia thumbnails. The full-size originals were not fetched. A file that was useful and not saved, or that is not openly licensed, is listed under "Not downloaded" with the URL only.

This folder is the south-bank interchange above the Low Level Bridge, and the span where Scona Road crosses Connors Road. The two Low Level river decks stay in the R13 notes. The James MacDonald river girder, and the one-lane ramp tagged toward South Scona Road, stay in the R12 notes.
