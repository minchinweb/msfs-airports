# Measurements from the LiDAR

Generated by `measure-sites.py` from the 2018 LiDAR clips (CGVD2013 metres). **Rise** = DSM minus DTM: the top surface (deck, rails, traffic) above bare ground. It is **not** the clear height under the deck: take off the deck depth (about 1 to 2.5 m, unknown per bridge). **Structure run** = the longest stretch of a mapped way whose rise exceeds 3 m, so its length is the measured length of the raised structure, ends included (the abutment fill counts). **Raised width** = how wide the raised surface at deck level is across the way, every deck and rail at that level side by side, so beside a parallel deck it is the whole structure, not one deck; 1 m pixels, +-1 m. Chainage is metres along the OSM way from its first node. Closed ways are measured as polygons (roofs and outlines). Profiles every 25 m are in `profiles.csv`.
