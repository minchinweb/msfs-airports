## What they say about our model (not yet applied)
- Layout, tower end to hangar end: tower annex, gap, **two-storey blue-grey office block** (~Aerocentre), a second two-storey block, a **long one-storey wing** of ribbed cladding, then the **tall cream hangar**. The one-storey wing and the second block are not in our model, which has only the Aerocentre and a separate cream box.
- Rough heights (scaling on the 1.9 m pickup and the ~3.6 m box truck; +/-1 m): one-storey wing eave ~4.5-5 m; two-storey blocks ~7.5-8.5 m (our Aerocentre 8.4 m is fine); tall cream hangar eave ~9-10 m to the red trim, ridge slightly more. Our hangar is 9 m, so perhaps 1 m low.
- The cream hangar is clad in pale ribbed metal with a red eave line, as modelled; the lower wings are blue-grey with yellow trim posts, not cream.
- The landside ground floor of the office block has a recessed drive-through bay under the upper floor.
