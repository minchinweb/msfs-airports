# CYXD SID plate text

Recovered procedure wording is the Canada Flight Supplement preferential-runway departure, dated with the 20 November 2008–15 January 2009 CFS. The named SID **EDMONTON City FOUR DEP (CYXD 4.)** is still only a title. No source retrieved in this pass prints its runway transitions, headings, radials, altitudes, climb gradient, or transitions.

## What is the full text of EDMONTON City FOUR DEP (CYXD 4.)?

### Takeaway

No plate, textual encoding, or leg list for EDMONTON City FOUR DEP was retrieved. The only procedure whose actual climb altitudes, runway order, and “before proceeding on course” instruction could be quoted is the CFS noise/preferential departure, which is not titled City FOUR.

### Cited Findings

- PilotNav’s procedure index for closed CYXD, fetched 13 October 2026, still lists the name only: “EDMONTON City FOUR DEP (CYXD 4.)”, grouped under “IAPs (Instrument Approach Procedures)” with Aerodrome Chart, three CFS chart titles, NDB Rwy 16 (GNSS), LOC (BC)/DME Rwy 16, LOC/DME Rwy 34, and ILS or NDB Rwy 34 (GNSS). The converted page has no runway transition, heading, altitude, radial, or effective date for that DEP. — [PilotNav CYXD](https://www.pilotnav.com/airport/CYXD)

### Inferences

- City FOUR sits in the same PilotNav index as the GNSS-overlay IAPs. The 11 February 2010 CAP 3 cycle is known to contain NDB RWY 16 (GNSS) at page 94. That places the *name* in the late civil procedure set. It does not date the SID, and it does not supply legs.
- The numeral FOUR is the usual Nav Canada amendment number for a procedure whose name is “City”. That is a naming inference only. It is not evidence of the path.

### Takeaway

## What take-off minima and DEP remarks are printed with the aerodrome?

### Takeaway

The 20 November 2008 CFS prints a full noise-abatement departure: runway priority, a runway-heading climb to stated altitudes before proceeding on course, and a night runway preference. It does not print a take-off visibility or RVR, and it does not use the title City FOUR. No CAP take-off minima box for CYXD was retrieved.

### Cited Findings

Quoted from Canada Flight Supplement / GPH 205, effective 0901Z 20 November 2008 to 0901Z 15 January 2009, pages B230–B231, text extraction of [CFS_AD_E.pdf](https://www.ivao.ca/charts/CFS/CFS_AD_E.pdf). Spelling and abbreviations are as extracted.

Header and identification (B230):

> CYXD EDMONTON CITY CENTRE (BLATCHFORD FIELD) AB
>
> REF N53 34 21 W113 31 14 Adj 17°E UTC-7(6) Elev 2200´ VTA A5015 E-15 E-16 LO2 HI3 T1 CAP
>
> OPR ATCO Frontec 780-477-2442 Cert Ldg fees
>
> PF A-1,2,3,6 B-5 C-4

The REF line includes “CAP”, meaning instrument procedures are published in the Canada Air Pilot. This block does not name which CAP charts.

Runways (B231):

Communications include “ARR DEP” Edmonton Terminal 119.5 and 226.3, clearance delivery 121.3, ground 121.9, tower 119.1 / 283.7. “DEP” here is the departure frequency label, not a procedure name.

PRO block, the departure procedure text (B231):

> Rgt hand circuits rwys 30 & 34 (CAR 602.96). Transponder rqrd in CZ. For VFR rtes see Edmonton VTPC or as directed by ATC. Do not descend below 3400 ASL until past the "XD NDB" when on a visual apch to rwy 16. Charter operators using acft larger than 19 seats require PPR 780-890-8213. Helicopters shall comply with Heli VTPC or as directed by NOISE ABATEMENT: ATC. Ab Initio Flying Tng not permitted within the CZ. Restrictions - Some acft ops are rstd btwn 2200-0700 lcl time, for info tel 780-477-2442. All acft are prohibited fr doing run-ups (exc run-ups done as part of pre-takeoff procedures) btwn 2200-0700 lcl time. Preferential Rwy Determination (H24) - Exc for purposes of ATC, the order of rwy priority shall be: Dep - Rwy 30, 34, 16 & 12. Acft climb on rwy hdg to 3200 ASL (4500 ASL for turbo-jets, exc 4000 ASL for rwy 30) BPOC. Arr - Rwy 12, 16, 30 & 34. Acft not to descend below 3200 ASL (4500 ASL for turbo-jets) until commencing final apch for ldg. All acft use rwys 12 & 30 as preferred rwys for arr & dep btwn 0000-0600 lcl time unless tfc, wind, weather, or rwy conditions require use of a less preferred rwy.

Page-date evidence in the same extraction: the volume header on these pages is 20 November 2008 to 15 January 2009. Immediately after the PRO block the extraction prints “issue 159” and “July 5 2007”, then the next aerodrome (Edmonton / Cooking Lake). Immediately after the B230 services block it prints “issue 159” and “20 November 2008”, then the B231 header. CFS page footers are amendment dates, but a two-column extraction can attach a footer to the wrong page. July 5 2007 is the date printed next to the end of the CYXD PRO; it is the best candidate for that page’s amendment date, not a separately verified amendment record.

No line in this CYXD block is labeled TAKE-OFF MINIMA, and no visibility or RVR value is printed. The ILS line ends with the word “RVR” and no number.

A secondary US flight-planning directory, not the CFS, says of City Centre (Blatchford Field): “Noise abatement: In effect; ctc 780/496-2836. Notes: Right tfc rys 30 & 34.” Coordinates in that entry are N53-34.21 W113-31.14. The file’s publication date was not on the extracted pages that mention CYXD. — [Airports of entry compilation](http://www.ilanreich.com/Public_Pics/Alaska/Airports%20of%20entry%20for%20international.pdf)

### Inferences

- “BPOC” in Canadian flight-supplement prose is the abbreviation “before proceeding on course”. The legend page that defines it was not opened in this pass; the operational sentence is still explicit: climb on runway heading until the stated altitude, then proceed on course.
- Altitudes are ASL. Field elevation in the same entry is 2,200 ft. The 3,200 ft ASL restriction is therefore 1,000 ft above the published field elevation. The turbojet figures are 4,500 ft ASL, except 4,000 ft ASL on runway 30. Those differences are arithmetic from the quoted numbers, not extra restrictions.
- This PRO is a noise and preferential-runway departure printed in the CFS. It is not the City FOUR SID. Nothing in the quote assigns a radial, a navaid to fly, or a transition name. The only lateral instruction on departure is “climb on rwy hdg” until the altitude, then BPOC.
- Runway priority for departure is 30, then 34, then 16, then 12, except between 0000 and 0600 local, when 12 and 30 are preferred for both arrival and departure unless traffic, wind, weather, or runway conditions require otherwise.
- Take-off visibility, if it was published, was on the CAP chart (the REF line points at CAP), not in this CFS block.

- The heli VTPC and the Edmonton VTPC graphics referenced by the PRO
