# CYXD reference links (collected 2026-10-12)

Index of every link the user collected (the old `_Next Steps.txt` scratch file, now retired), grouped by subject. Open questions and the to-do list are in `open-questions.md`. "Saved" = image copied into `docs/cyxd/...` (see the folder's SOURCES.md or this
page for credit). "Link only" = not fetched; the reason is given. JetPhotos, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit and the Ingenium and city of Edmonton
sites do not serve images or text to scripts (blocked, login or script-rendered), so those stay as links; open them yourself.

## Hangar 11 (Northwest Industries, 1956)
- Saved: `hangar11-photos/flickr_woodysworld1_11393738936.jpg` (woodysworld1778; the 1956 Northwest Industries Ltd / Commercial Aircraft Service building: three-storey
  white block with a rooftop control cab and a big roof sign, barrel-vault hangar behind it at the right, low wing with dark doors at the left). It matches Hangar 11's
  barrel vault and south annex, so it is modelled in this form.
- Saved: `hangar11-photos/flickr_woodhead_11094833594.jpg` (high view over Hangar 11 and the long two-storey building north of it).
