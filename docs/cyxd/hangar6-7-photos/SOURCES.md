## Roundels and gable direction (read from the photos; not confirmed)
- **Hangar 6**: the "6" roundel is on the long pale blue-grey wall (left face in the photo), high up, near the corner shared with the green wall; the sign board and door are on the same wall. That wall's top edge has navy trim and steps down at the far end, so it is likely the rake (gable) side, with the ridge running perpendicular to it, i.e. along the green wall's length. The green wall has plain white fascia, so it is probably the eave wall. Which way the gable runs is the least certain point.
- **Hangar 7**: in the background of this photo the small "7" roundel is on the wall facing the camera, near its left end. In `../hangar7-photos/` the roundel is on the corner wall at the left of the Ody photo (flat cornice at the top, so seen on a flat-topped wall), and "HANGAR 7" lettering plus the glazed lower wall is on the other face (Blatherwick photo, flat top edge). Neither photo shows a pitched gable end, so the gable direction for Hangar 7 is unknown.

### User correction (2026-10-10)
In `EDMONTON_MUNI__11094165595_.jpg` the gable ends of **both** Hangar 6 and Hangar 7 face the left side of the photo, so the gable end of Hangar 6 is the pale blue-grey wall that carries the "6" roundel, and Hangar 7's gable end faces the same way (left). The compass orientation is not known yet; check it against the OSM outlines or the aerial when we build them.

Colours of the three hangars visible (winter light, so tones may be washed out):
- **Foreground, the "RCMP hangar" (most likely Hangar 7):** pale steel-blue / blue-grey vertical-ribbed cladding on the long wall, a darker slate-blue block at its right end with an awning, wall lamps and a small entrance; a pale low-slope roof with vent stacks. Long wall parallel to the car park; the short end at the left faces the airfield.
- **Behind it, Hangar 6 (white):** white. A white flat-roofed lower block with a blue door and a company logo, and a taller white block behind with a row of dark windows. This is the same white wall seen in `EDMONTON_MUNI__11094165595_.jpg`; the green end wall from that photo is not visible from here.
- **Further right (northeast), the next hangar (probably Hangar 5):** dark charcoal / bronze-brown, with a continuous dark glazed band under a lighter grey roof, which fits the bronze curtain-wall office block in `../hangar5-photos/`. Beyond it, a tan/cream low block and a blue-grey roof at the far right edge (probably Hangar 4's neighbours).

Does the foreground hangar match Hangar 7? Probably, with two caveats:
- It matches the blue ribbed wall and the darker blue annex seen with the "7" roundel behind Hangar 6 in `EDMONTON_MUNI__11094165595_.jpg`, and the order (7 in front, 6 and then 5 receding along the row) agrees with the airside photo in `../hangar7-photos/EDMONTON_MUNI__13858359315_.jpg`.
- The Ody/Blatherwick photos show a teal-green tint (1980s film), and this winter shot shows a greyer steel-blue. Treat the colour as steel-blue with a green tint. No roundel is visible from this side.
- The "gable end faces the apron" note may conflict: here the long ribbed wall runs parallel to the car park edge, with its short end towards the airfield. Check the ridge direction on an aerial before modelling.
