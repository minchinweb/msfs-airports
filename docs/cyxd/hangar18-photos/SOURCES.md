# CYXD Hangar 18 (Edmonton Flying Club) - reference photos

Saved 2026-10-10 for a later Hangar 18 model. Hangar 18 stands immediately north of the control tower and housed the Edmonton Flying Club. It is **not a simple box hangar**: it is a composite of a tall two-storey office/clubhouse block, a green-clad hangar with white sliding doors, and a side bay with a striped wall.

## What the photos show (for the model)
- **Not a simple box.** Roughly three parts: (1) the white two-storey landside clubhouse with the observation room and balcony; (2) the green ribbed hangar with the two white door leaves facing airside; (3) a lower wing with a striped (cream/mauve/tan) upper wall and green lower wall with window slots, set at a right angle to the green hangar.
- Colours: white (clubhouse and front), dark green (hangar cladding), cream concrete-block piers, tan/beige corrugated fascia.
- "18" roundel: on the landside clubhouse block high above the shuttered panel (Commons photo), and on the airside end wall at the top left in the JetPhotos shot.
- Livery on the white airside wall: a green and red-orange curved swoosh stripe.
- This is the post-1972 look. For the 1940-1985 range the earlier hangar here would have to be checked against aerials.

## What the airside video shows (woodysworld1778, 14 s pan, winter, reviewed 2026-10-10)
Frames in order, panning from the control tower toward the north:
1. **Tower and clubhouse.** The control tower is at the left edge (shaft with the blue "17" roundel, a door with steps, a bracket lamp). The Edmonton Flying Club clubhouse stands just beyond it across a small gap: an airside **two-storey flat-roofed building with rounded/curved corners**, a deep overhanging dark roof fascia carrying the lettering "EDMONTON FLYING CLUB", a continuous glazed upper floor with a balcony railing in front, a white lower floor with glazed doors, and roof lights and antennas. A green door of the neighbouring hangar shows at its right. An orange barrel and a block lie in front.
2. **Hangar front.** To the right of the clubhouse: the green ribbed hangar, with two white sliding door leaves with small windows, a lower cream-brick annex with a blue sign on its left, and the Ramada hotel behind.
3. **White end wall.** Right of the green hangar: a long, tall, flat white wall (the hangar's end/side) with a **green and red-orange swoosh stripe** running along it that curves up at the left, and the "18" roundel high at the right-hand corner. A cream brick pier with a personnel door separates the green face from the white wall.
4. Further north the view continues past the white hangar to lower brown/cream buildings along the apron edge (the next hangars), then open apron.

Takeaways for the model: (a) the clubhouse is a separate low rounded-corner building between the tower and the hangar, on the airside, with the club name on its fascia; (b) the green-fronted hangar is next to it, with the white swoosh-striped wing at its north end carrying the "18" roundel; (c) all of these are from the 2010s, so check older aerials for the 1940-1985 look.
