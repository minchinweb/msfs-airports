# Hangar 39 - PWA-era reference search (2026-10-21)

Folder placed beside the other `docs/cyxd/*-photos/` folders (there is no `reference/` folder in this repo).

## Summary of findings

**No PWA-era (1950s-1980s) photograph of Hangar 39 was found, and no image was downloaded.** Nothing in this folder is an image.

- The only freely licensed Hangar 39 photos online are Jason Woodhead's 2014 shots (Commons, CC BY 2.0). They are already in `../hangar39-photos/` (a Commons search for "PWA hangar Edmonton" returns only those five files). They show the post-PWA paint scheme and the airport-era "39" roundel. They do not show PWA signage.
- Web searches (Wikipedia, City of Edmonton heritage pages, Alberta HeRMIS, Alberta Aviation Museum, Flickr, JetPhotos, Commons) found no description of the PWA face: no door signage, logo, roundel or paint colours. The Flickr text search returned nothing for "pacific western hangar edmonton".
- Not verified: a purpose-built PWA hangar at Edmonton Municipal. The only documented PWA tenancy found is the lease of No. 6 Hangar (now Hangar 14, the Alberta Aviation Museum) from 1958 to 1960 (City of Edmonton "Historical Significance of Hangar 14"). The link between "Hangar 39" and PWA rests on the 2014 captions "old PWA hanger" and is not confirmed by any archive source I could reach.
- So the PWA-era face of Hangar 39 cannot be described from evidence. The best available is the 2014 appearance (pale blue and white ribbed cladding, blue base band, two door bays, "39" roundel), and that may be a later repaint.
