# Hangar #2 - reference photos

From the City of Edmonton Archives (reference only; do not redistribute).

- `city-edmonton-archives-undated-EA-275-1121.jpg`: "Trans-Canada Air Lines, Hanger 2" (archive page `trans-canada-air-lines-hanger-2`). Front of a hangar with a segmental-arch parapet carrying TRANS-CANADA AIR LINES lettering and the TCA roundel, a long open front under a corrugated canopy, two rows of paned windows in the back wall, an airliner inside. Date unknown (TCA was founded in 1937). Which of the later arched hangars this is has not been checked against the 1930s-40s photos (`../hangar1938-photos/`, `../hangar1930-photos/`).
