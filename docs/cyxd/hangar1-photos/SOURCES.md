# Hangar #1 (the 1927 wooden hangar: board-and-batten walls, hipped peak roof, wide front opening) - reference photos

All from the City of Edmonton Archives (reference only; do not redistribute). Not to be confused with the stucco "first hangar" with the tower in `../hangar1930-photos/`, which came later.

- `city-edmonton-archives-1927-01-07-EA-10-3181-18.jpg`: newspaper-style panel for the opening of the airport, 7 Jan 1927; the bottom right inset shows Siskins in front of "McNeill's old hangar" with the peaked roof.
- `city-edmonton-archives-1929-EA-10-2756.jpg`: front view with an aircraft (G-CYYD) beside it, a staircase against the side of the front opening, wooden board walls, low annex at the left.
- `city-edmonton-archives-undated-EA-10-1251.jpg`: biplane (J-7758) in the open front, winter; board walls each side of a central pyramid-roofed section. The City Archives site titles it "Hanger #1" and lists 2018, which is the digitisation date, not when the photo was taken, so the file stays `undated`.
- `city-edmonton-archives-1929-EA-10-3181-54-2.jpg`: scrapbook panel, "In 1929, it looked like this...": four Flying Club Moths and the Commercial Airways Vega on the field, with the small peaked-roof wooden hangar in the background. The City Archives page is `blatchford-field-3`; the photo is dated 1929.
