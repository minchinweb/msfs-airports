## Review of the hangars already built
Changes made today (built OK, not yet viewed in the sim):
- **Hangar 4** now faces the runway (heading 270). The Wikimedia photo shows a 28 m arched opening, a "4" roundel and a Government of Alberta sign. I put those on the west long wall (71 m, ridge along N-S, eaves 9 m so the arch fits). Brick block at the north end, orange panels at the south end (8 m, so it stays off Hangar 5). **This is my interpretation**: the OSM outline is 71 m N-S x 33.5 m, so a runway-facing door means the opening is in the long wall, not a gable. Please check against what you know.
- **Hangars 38 and 39** are much taller and side by side: 38 walls 12.5 m (ridge 15.7 m), 39 main bay walls 14 m (ridge 17.6 m), low bay 10.4 m, rear wing 9 m; doors, louvres, roundels and the "38" roundel (now 1.4 m radius, high on the corner) were scaled to suit and tall narrow windows run along 38's long walls. Hangar 39 moved from OSM way 230718900 to beside 38: fronts flush, 4 m gap, so its centre is 44 m east and 50 m north of 38's (it was 186 m north-north-west). The 114 x 97 m double hall that used to be between them was never built.
- **Hangar 5**: lower wall is now charcoal-brown (was tan), under the cream band, as in photos 48-51.
- **Hangar 18**: landside (NW) face of the club block now has white pilasters, a sign strip and the blue "18" roundel at the upper right (photo 3).

Findings not yet applied (everything is small detail):
- **Hangar 5**: photo 49 shows the top of the landside end wall sloping, i.e. a gable end faces 109 St; the model has the doors on the west gable and the office on the east. The "5" roundel is high at the left corner of the long wall (the model has it on the south wall; roughly right).
- **Hangar 7**: a row of small white awnings over the windows and a blue/gold crest sign on the long wall facing 109 St (photos 54-56) are missing. The annex is white, not slate.
- **Hangar 8**: the tall end block carries the "8" and the LAW OFFICE AVIATION sign (photo 59); it has riveted white siding and a continuous glazed clerestory. In photo 61 the large glazed door is at the SOUTH end of the long wall; check which end the tall block is on (the model puts it at the north-west).
- **Hangar 11**: confirmed (barrel vault, brick-faced lower wall, chimney annex, three-storey clapboard annex with fire escape and U-HAUL doors, NAIT parkade behind). Photos 62-67 are a good basis for a window/door pass on the annex.
- **Hangar 15** (photo 1): the "15" roundel sits high on the landside wall at the left end, windows are small and square, there is a blue awning over the office door, and AVIONICS lettering on the parapet behind. The model has the roundels on the gable ends.
- **Hangar 18 footprint**: **partly confirmed.** The street-level views show the clubhouse (two storeys, balcony) at the tower end, a long pilastered white landside facade north of the entrance about 45 m or more long (photo 4), and further white blocks at the north end (photos 5, 6). That fits the hand-traced 60 x 43 m footprint (plus the rear wing), but a long facade of 45 m or more is longer than the model's 20 m club block. I did not re-trace the footprint: the 2014 aerial is the only measuring source and would need a closer look at the north end.
- **Hangar 39**: no roundel is visible in the Street View photos; the photos show one pale-blue main building with 2-3 stepped roof levels and white office wings on the west side, not the two clean bays the model has.

## Aircraft on pedestals in front of the Alberta Aviation Museum
Three aircraft, not four (the "white jet" seen past the Norseman in photo 78 is the Voodoo): from the north the Bomarc 60447, the Norseman CF-HPY on a green pylon, a memorial, and the CF-101B Voodoo 101060 on a blue pole. Identified from the museum's collection pages and OSM; the 2026-10-21 photos (`hangar14-photos/frontage/`) cover all of them from several sides, including the Voodoo's underside. **Built** as `cyxd_h14_bomarc`, `cyxd_h14_norseman`, `cyxd_h14_memorial`, `cyxd_h14_voodoo` (`tools/display_aircraft.py`, placed at the OSM nodes). Markings are roundels and flag only; no lettering or serial numbers. Pole heights and tilt angles are estimates from the photos.

## Building 21 (old terminal) and stands
Photos 13 and 14 are the old terminal (OSM way 230718907, 101 Airport Rd, Amiskwaciy Academy). In the 2014 Esri imagery four pale strips (patched pavement at the jetway positions) run out from its airside face; `footprints/terminal21-stands-overlay.png` shows the four stands over that image. In `CYXD.xml`:
- Gate 7 (parking 79) was moved from the apron to the west and re-headed to the northernmost stand; its jetway was moved with it (Megalink to Link). It was the only existing parking overlapping a new stand (gates 8, 9 are 65-130 m away and stay).
- New GATE 10, 11, 12 (parking 118-120, GATE_SMALL, radius 15 m) with Link jetways at the other three strips, nose-in with the aircraft heading toward the building, parking point 25 m from the jetway root. Headings 136, 232, 228, 262 degrees follow the strips.
- PARKING paths 22 -> 10, 22 -> 11 and 23 -> 12 (not drawn); gate 7 keeps its path from node 85. A new apron polygon (about 90 x 70 m, same surface) covers the stand area.
- Not modelled: the terminal building itself (reference only so far), and the parking garage.
- Assumptions: bridge length 25 m, no check of the jetway object's alignment in the sim. The jetway origin convention was copied from gate 8 (object heading = parking heading), so watch whether the bridge extends toward the aircraft.
