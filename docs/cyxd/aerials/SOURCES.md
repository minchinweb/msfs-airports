# Aerial photos and maps

## City of Edmonton Archives (added 2026-10-22; reference only, do not redistribute)
The date is in the file name, the archive ID after it. Dates are the archive's folder dates.
- `city-edmonton-archives-1945-EA-160-260.jpg`, `...-1948-10-06-EA-10-2344.jpg`, `...-1960-EA-10-3135.jpg`, `...-1960-EA-10-3136.jpg`: vertical and oblique views of the whole airfield.
- `city-edmonton-archives-1982-ET-28-897.jpg`, `...-899.jpg`, `...-900.jpg`: oblique views of the east line, the terminal and the Kingsway hangars (900 is the source for the second, orange, hangar).
- `city-edmonton-archives-1980ca-ET-28-895.jpg`, `...-906.jpg`: oblique views from the tower side.
- `city-edmonton-archives-1962-canvas.png`, `...-1962-EAM-62.pdf`: 1962 maps.

## Other
- `national-archives-1944-RG-18-AA-208-27-ac.jpg`: 1944 National Archives aerial of the airfield (moved from `National Archives/`). Basis for the historic building placements.

- `ualberta-archive-org-1937-WCWdb_2534-general-plan.pdf` (moved from `ualberta-archive-photos/`, saved 2026-10-17):
https://archive.org/details/WCWdb_2534 | "Edmonton Municipal Airport, General Plan showing development to meet Air Regulations, 1937." City Engineer's Office, Edmonton. Dated 7 April 1937, amended to 14 June 1943. University of Alberta Map Collection (WCW). No rights statement given. | Blueprint, scale 200 ft : 1 in, single page (9.5 MB). Shows the runway strips with surface notes and dates, the circular drive, hangars Nos. 1, 2 and 3, the A.O.S. (RCAF) buildings, Kingsway, the north plant sheds and double hangar, drainage, power lines and the field boundary. The amendment table lists changes up to 1943. Magnetic variation noted at the lower left. Faded and stained; the OCR text is on archive.org (`WCWdb_2534_djvu.txt`). 

  Possible use: a dated plan to check runway and building positions against the 1944 aerial and the runway-04 alignment question in `STATUS.md`. Not georeferenced; nothing has been measured from it yet.
