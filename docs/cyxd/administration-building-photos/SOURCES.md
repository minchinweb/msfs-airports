# Administration Building and terminal - University of Alberta Libraries postcards (archive.org)

Saved 2026-10-17, filed here 2026-10-22. Both are from the University of Alberta Libraries collections on the Internet Archive.

| `ualberta-archive-org-PC013720.jpg` | https://archive.org/details/PC013720 | "Administration Buildings at Municipal Airport in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada". Northern Photo Services, Ltd (publisher). Dated 1948-1988 in the metadata. Peel's Prairie Provinces postcard collection. Rights: Copyright Not Evaluated. | Colour aerial postcard, looking south-east. Three-storey Administration Building: centre block with a control-tower cab, two-storey wings to each side with flat roofs, facing the apron. Circular landscaped drive and flower beds in front, car parks to each side, red/white striped masts at the wing ends, an X-shaped runway/taxiway intersection behind. Cars look 1950s. A multi-engine airliner is parked at the apron. |

A DC-3-type airliner is on the apron. **The "1929" date cannot be right**: the barrel-vault hangars and the terminal date from 1938 or later. Treat it as c.1940s. |
