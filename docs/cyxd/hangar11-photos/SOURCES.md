## Background (from the articles)
- **Forgotten Edmonton** https://www.forgottenedmonton.com/blog/hangar-11 : 11760 109 Street, constructed 1942-43; the US Army Air Force's "Hangar T3", one of four USAAF hangars built along the **eastern edge** of Blatchford Field, apart from the RCAF facilities. Preliminary construction began in November 1942 with no detailed drawings. A heritage report by ERA Architects is referenced.
- **Edmonton Journal**, "Hangar 11 endangered..." https://edmontonjournal.com/news/local-news/hangar-11-endangered-city-says-historic-building-might-be-too-costly-to-retain : the 1942 hangar, 7,400 m2, the last remaining WWII hangar on the former City Centre Airport site (Hangar 14, 1941, is the Alberta Aviation Museum, outside the Blatchford site); restoration estimated at $14-20 million.
- **Global News** https://globalnews.ca/news/7613428/hangar-11-edmonton-historic-development/ (1 Feb 2021): boarded up since 2013, water coming in, the city weighing a sale; repair costs quoted as $18-83 million.
- **DCN-JOC / ConstructConnect** https://canada.constructconnect.com/joc/news/infrastructure/2022/05/edmonton-wartime-hangar-declared-historic-resource (26 May 2022): Municipal Historic Resource; $5 million grant over 10 years; new owners planned mixed-use / student housing; built by the USAAF in 1942, part of the Lend-Lease programme.
- **Edmonton Journal**, "Fire destroys Edmonton's Hangar 11, Happy Valley-Goose Bay's Hangar 8" https://edmontonjournal.com/news/local-news/fire-destroys-edmonton-hangar-11-happy-valley-goose-bay-hangar-8 : fire on Monday 22 April 2024; authorities believed it may have been deliberately set ("suspicious").

## Notes for the model
- Era: built 1942-43 so it belongs in the 1940-1985 scenery. The 2010s details (plywood boarding, "Student parking", "Heated parking", U-Haul / "For lease" signs, "11" roundel) are late additions; the "11" roundel matches the other end-of-life numbering. In the scenery period the doors would have been in use; the two-storey/three-storey annexes and the barrel roof are original.
- Form: long **barrel-vault hangar**, white-clad roof and upper walls; tan/peach large sliding doors on the airside (towards the apron; compass side not confirmed); low brick/panel lower storeys along the landside; a taller white **three-storey annex** at the south end with an external steel fire escape and a smaller attached block at its front; a two-storey tower-room annex at the north end.
- The gable/door orientation and exact dimensions are not confirmed; check OSM and the existing generated footprint before modelling.
