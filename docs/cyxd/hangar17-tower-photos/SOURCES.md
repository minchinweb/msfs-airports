## What the photos show (used for the model)
- Octagonal shaft (rectangle with chamfered corners, about 10 x 11 m), 7 floors: grey lower 5 floors, a salmon/red band (1 floor), white top floor; 2 small windows per face per floor; steel stair cage up one side.
- Cab: hexagonal/octagonal glazing leaning outward, red-orange fascia and roof, balcony with rail around the shaft top and a rail on the cab roof, several whip antennas.
- Blue disc with yellow "17" on the grey shaft. The same disc numbering (e.g. "18") was on other buildings at the end.
- Low flat-roofed grey NAV CANADA annex wrapped around the base.
