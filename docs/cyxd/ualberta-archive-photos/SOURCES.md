## Possible uses
- `PC013720`: the 1950 City Archives photo EA-10-838 already shows the same Administration Building; this one is a clearer colour view of the wings, roofs and drive for the historic building model.
- `PC013987`: the two-hangar group and towers match EA-160-398 / EA-160-402 (1938); the curved terminal and apron are a better view than the 1944 aerial.
- `WCWdb_2534.pdf`: a dated plan to check runway and building positions against the 1944 aerial and the runway-04 alignment question in `STATUS.md`. Not georeferenced; nothing has been measured from it yet.
