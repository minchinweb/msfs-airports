# CYXD Hangar 8 - reference photos

Saved 2026-10-10 for a later Hangar 8 model (1920 px Commons renditions). Both are aircraft photos (an abandoned Piper Cheyenne, C-GRBE) with the hangar in the background; the Commons upload dates read 2000-01-01 (placeholder), likely spring 2014.

| `EDMONTON_MUNI__13858703564_.jpg` | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:EDMONTON_MUNI_(13858703564).jpg | jasonwoodhead23, CC BY 2.0 (Flickr original: https://flickr.com/photos/80365963@N00/13858703564) | **Best view.** Hangar 8 behind the aircraft: a pale grey/blue-grey hangar with a gable end at the left and a long, low side wing running right. The gable (doors) face is a wide glazed sliding-door wall (small-paned wire-glass windows, dark guide posts) under a grey canopy roof with a green trim line, gently pitched roof above. At the right a taller, flat-topped, plain pale-grey end block carries the blue "8" roundel (yellow numeral) high up at its top corner, with a dark downpipe or stack beside it. Past that the long wing continues to the right with a clerestory band of glazing along the roof and doors/windows below. The NAIT parkade stands behind at left. |
| `EDMONTON_MUNI__13858738284_.jpg` | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:EDMONTON_MUNI_(13858738284).jpg | jasonwoodhead23, CC BY 2.0 (Flickr original: https://flickr.com/photos/80365963@N00/13858738284; Commons caption "Abandoned aircraft at old muni airport NAIT parkade in background") | Only a sliver of Hangar 8 is visible at the far right edge: the corner of a grey-green ribbed block with a tall glazed bay under a roof band, next to the NAIT parkade. Useful mainly for placement (the hangar sits just west of the parkade in this view) and the colour of the cladding. |
