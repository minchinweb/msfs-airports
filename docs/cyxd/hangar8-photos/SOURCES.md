# CYXD Hangar 8 - reference photos

Saved 2026-10-10 for a later Hangar 8 model (1920 px Commons renditions). Both are aircraft photos (an abandoned Piper Cheyenne, C-GRBE) with the hangar in the background; the Commons upload dates read 2000-01-01 (placeholder), likely spring 2014.

## Moved here from `hangar9-photos/` (2026-10-21): the "Hangar 9" photos are Hangar 8

The user confirmed on 2026-10-21 that the photos filed as "Hangar 9" show Hangar 8 (same full-width glazed sliding-door wall under a canopy with a green fascia, tall flat-topped end block with a ladder at the right, lower annex at the left). Hangar 9 has no photo and no footprint.
