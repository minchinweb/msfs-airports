# Official SIDs, CAP, CFS — CYXD

## Bottom line

No 1940–1980 Canada Air Pilot SID plate with recoverable track, altitude, or navaid text was found in public sources. One later named SID is attested: **EDMONTON City FOUR DEP (CYXD 4.)**.

## Named SID

- **Name:** EDMONTON City FOUR DEP
- **Computer ident:** CYXD 4. / CYXD4
- **Source:** PilotNav procedure index for closed CYXD (https://www.pilotnav.com/airport/CYXD)
- **What is missing:** runway transitions, climb gradients, altitude restrictions, navaid radials, transitions, and effective dates. No graphic or textual plate was located on FlightAware, AirNav, Nav Canada current products, or public CAP PDFs.
- **Interpretation:** Nav Canada SID titles of the form CITY FOUR mean the fourth amendment of a “City” departure. That naming is typical of late conventional (or early RNAV) CAP SIDs, not 1940s radio-range practice. It is evidence a SID existed in the CAP at some point before closure (2013), not that it existed in 1940–1980.

FlightAware’s current CYXD procedure page lists **no** plates (airport closed).

CAP 3 purpose statement (Nav Canada eCAP3 product page): CAP contains IAPs, **diverse and standard instrument departures (SID)**, STARs, noise abatement, visual approaches, aerodrome/taxi charts. So if City FOUR existed, it lived in CAP 3 (Prairie volume), not the CFS.

## CFS / take-off minima

- CFS 20 Nov 2008–15 Jan 2009 (public cadet PDF) indexes **CYXD Edmonton City Centre (Blatchford Field) AB** but the posted file is general pages only — the Alberta aerodrome directory pages were not in that extract.
- Wikipedia cites CFS for field data (elev 2202 ft, RWY 12/30 5870×200, 16/34 5700×200) and states curfew/noise existed; it does **not** quote DEP or TAKE-OFF MINIMA text.
- Canadian default IFR departure when no SID/ODP is assigned (AIM / AvCanada discussion of CAP GEN): 35 ft by DER, climb runway heading, no turn below 400 AGL, 200 ft/NM until IFR altitude, then on course or as cleared. This is the legally expected behaviour at CYXD whenever a SID was not assigned.

## Noise law (departure-related, not a SID)

CAR 602.105: must follow noise abatement in CAP/CFS, including **departure procedures**, preferential runways, restricted hours.

CAR 602.106 table (still listing the closed airport in archived Justice pages): **Edmonton City Centre (Blatchford Field) — All runways** are noise-restricted for take-off of subsonic turbojets MCTOW > 34 000 kg unless a noise-compliance certificate is aboard (with emergency exceptions). This is a **heavy-jet take-off ban/restriction**, not a heading procedure.

Secondary sources: strictest noise 22:00–07:00 local; field remained 24/7 (medevac etc.).
